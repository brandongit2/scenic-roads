# 3D buildings research index: where open building heights, storeys and models beat Overture

Index of the six regional buildings reports (board item i1328 and the five before it, run 2026-11-13). Research only. The source table was written by hand from each report's full region tables and overlap sections, with the figures copied as the report states them; this file adds no measurement. Every table row was checked for its header's cell count. The reports are buildings-usa.md (USA: federal, states, cities, territories), buildings-americas.md (Canada, Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean, South America), buildings-europe-w.md (UK, Ireland, Benelux, France, Switzerland, Austria, Iberia, Italy, Slovenia to Cyprus), buildings-europe-e.md (Germany, the Nordics and Baltics, Poland to Serbia, Ukraine, Belarus, Russia, the Caucasus, Turkey), buildings-asia.md (East, South-East, Central and South Asia, the Middle East) and buildings-rest.md (Africa, Oceania, Antarctica and the global datasets, including a comparison of the global height sets against trusted heights in four boxes). The USA, Americas, Europe and Asia reports sampled Overture release 2026-09-23.1; REST sampled 2026-10-21.0, so shares are not comparable box for box.

## 1. Summary by area: what exists beyond Overture

- **The one global pattern.** Overture's heights are OSM tags, plus Microsoft ML estimates in some countries and, in the USA, patchy "USGS Lidar" values; outside the places below most buildings have neither height nor storeys (Cologne 49.5 %, Istanbul 97.5 %, Ankara 93.5 % with neither: EUROPE-E section 9; Accra 89.0 %, Dakar 99.6 %, Luanda 99.6 %, Melbourne 80.3 %, Auckland 92.1 %, Christchurch 92.9 %, Suva 98.0 %: REST section 8). Measured, open sources that add a height or storeys exist for a limited set of countries and cities.
- **USA.** Nothing national beats Overture's measured heights. Two lidar levers: NGA's "133 US cities" buildings (public domain, 2004-2015) and FEMA/ORNL USA Structures (CC BY 4.0), whose lidar-mean height covers 31,926,171 of 131,811,952 GeoParquet rows (24.2 %). Cities and counties with lidar heights: NYC (1,082,255 of 1,082,972), Denver, Houston, LA County (3,089,192 of 3,293,177), Texas TxGIO (2,185,538 in 80 of 254 counties), Rhode Island. Overture's US height share runs from 98.5 % (NYC, an OSM import of the city layer) to 50 % (rural Iowa, Microsoft only).
- **Canada.** NRCan's Automatically Extracted Buildings (12,393,262 lidar footprints with `heightmax`, OGL-Canada, 2.4 GB GeoParquet) is the one national measured-height layer in the Americas and Overture does not carry it; in the Toronto box 68.9 % of NRCan buildings add a height. Toronto, Calgary, Edmonton and Montréal add detail.
- **Mexico to South America.** Storeys or heights from a few cities (Bogotá storeys add to 92.9 % of Overture's buildings; Rio 65.4 %; Belo Horizonte, Brasília, Buenos Aires, Guadalajara, Mexico City) and IGN's BD TOPO for the French Antilles and Guiana. São Paulo's layer adds almost nothing (already imported through OSM) and is CC BY-SA 4.0. No open LoD2 city model with attributes in South America.
- **Europe, west.** The richest region: France's BD TOPO (51.5 M buildings), Brussels, Luxembourg, Switzerland (swissBUILDINGS3D, Zurich, Bern, Geneva), Austria (BEV, Tyrol, Vienna), Rotterdam, Milan and Lombardy, Bologna, Zagreb, Antwerp; storeys from Spain's Catastro, Switzerland's register, Slovenia and Cyprus. The UK's OS height products are not open; London's Building Stock Model is the open exception. 3DBAG is already inside Overture where the Amsterdam import ran.
- **Europe, north and east.** Germany's measured LoD2 is open Land by Land (Cologne/NRW 95.7 % of source buildings add a height, Munich/Bayern 86.5 %, Berlin Mitte 81.6 %, Hannover 70.2 %; Brandenburg adds 7.9 % because Overture already holds equal heights); BKG's federal products are restricted. Estonia's LoD2, Riga's LoD1, Slovakia's INSPIRE buildings (83 % of Bratislava's), Brno's heights, Czech storeys and Poland's GUGiK LoD2 (236 of 380 counties) are open. Turkey, Ukraine, Belarus, Russia and the Caucasus have no open per-building height or storey source.
- **Asia and the Middle East.** Japan's PLATEAU (306 units) is the best open set in Asia; Hong Kong's building parts, Seoul, Taichung and Taipei storeys, Singapore's HDB, Abu Dhabi, Beer Sheva, Cyprus and China's CMAB add heights or storeys in places. India, Mongolia and most of South-East and Central Asia: nothing open found.
- **Africa.** No open national or city height, storey or 3D source for any country apart from IGN's BD TOPO for Réunion and Mayotte, the City of Cape Town's two layers (non-commercial) and Kigali's EO4Kigali (coarse); eThekwini's footprints carry no height. 3D-GloBFP covers every African country and territory (including Morocco, Western Sahara and Libya, where Google 2.5D does not) and gives a height to 49 % to 99 % of Overture's height-less buildings in 12 of 13 boxes; Google 2.5D is closest in the one box with a trusted source (Cape Town).
- **Oceania.** Queensland's LiDAR outlines (25.3 % of Overture's buildings in the Brisbane box, 17.7 % on the Gold Coast), City of Melbourne (37.9 %), Launceston (65.8 %), Ballarat (22.4 %), the City of Adelaide's 3D model (71 %, indicative), Christchurch (65 %) and Queenstown Lakes (74 %). Geoscape (19,159,278 buildings, 82.1 % with a height record), Vicmap Buildings and NSW's supply are commercial or government-only; LINZ's national outlines have no height; Wellington's footprints carry an elevation, not a height. The Pacific islands: no open source with heights.
- **Global fallbacks (REST section 9.5, four boxes against trusted heights).** Overture's own Microsoft ML heights are the closest where they exist; GlobalBuildingAtlas LoD1 (one Berlin tile: 3.08 m low, ratio 0.84, correlation 0.60; CC BY-NC) next in Europe; 3D-GloBFP overestimates in dense centres (+8.29 m Berlin Mitte, +6.41 m Melbourne, +43.69 m Midtown Manhattan on block-sized polygons) and has no bias in Cape Town (+0.43 m); GHS-BUILT-H is a 100 m average; Google 2.5D is closest in Cape Town (median absolute 2.24 m, correlation 0.84).

## 3. Recommended order of use (a proposal, not a decision)

1. **Measured, open sources first, by region.** Germany's Länder LoD2 (Cologne 95.7 %, Munich 86.5 %, Berlin Mitte 81.6 % of source buildings add a height); France's BD TOPO; Japan's PLATEAU (Tokyo 98.3 %); Canada's NRCan set (Toronto 68.9 %); Queensland's LiDAR outlines, City of Melbourne, Launceston and Ballarat; Christchurch and Queenstown Lakes; Brussels, Luxembourg, Switzerland, Austria, Rotterdam, Milan, Zagreb, Slovakia and Brno; then USA's lidar city layers (NGA's 133 cities through USA Structures) and NYC.
2. **Storeys where heights are missing:** Spain's Catastro (Madrid 70.6 %), Czechia's RÚIAN, Denmark's BBR, Bogotá (92.9 %), Seoul, Taichung, Singapore, Abu Dhabi, Cyprus, Beer Sheva, Slovenia.
3. **Overture's Microsoft heights where present.** They were the closest building-level value in Berlin Mitte (median absolute 3.97 m, ratio 0.84, 64.4 % within 20 %) and central Melbourne (2.45 m, 0.88, 31.4 %), but exist on 13.5 % to 17.1 % of the trusted buildings there.
4. **A global fallback for the rest, by continent (REST section 9.5, errors against trusted heights).**
   - **Europe, Australia, New Zealand:** GlobalBuildingAtlas LoD1 where the owner accepts its non-commercial licence (Berlin Mitte: median absolute 6.75 m, signed -3.08 m); otherwise 3D-GloBFP (Berlin Mitte +8.29 m, ratio 1.65; Melbourne +6.41 m, ratio 1.92: it overestimates in dense centres); GHS-BUILT-H last (ratios 0.85 Berlin, 2.13 Melbourne).
   - **North America:** Overture's OSM-imported or Microsoft heights; 3D-GloBFP's block-sized polygons ran +43.69 m (ratio 3.12) in Midtown Manhattan and GHS-BUILT-H 1.27 times, so neither is a building-level fallback there.
   - **Africa, South and South-East Asia, Latin America:** Google 2.5D Temporal where it covers (Cape Town: median absolute 2.24 m, ratio 0.80, correlation 0.84); 3D-GloBFP everywhere else in Africa (it is the only global per-building height for Morocco, Western Sahara and Libya; median ratio against OSM heights 0.40 to 2.18 across 13 boxes; no bias in Cape Town, +0.43 m).
5. **Keep one height definition per layer** (roof ridge, gutter, DSM minus terrain; Queensland's ridge heights run 1.6 to 1.8 times Overture's, eave heights 0.91; Europe-W measured ratios of 1.0 to 2.2) and keep CC BY sets as a separate attributed layer (the OSM wiki rates CC BY "likely incompatible" for contributing into OSM itself, a community page).

## 4. Where the reports differ

- **Overture release:** USA, Americas, Europe and Asia sampled 2026-09-23.1; REST sampled 2026-10-21.0 (the USA report says the October release changed nothing for buildings).
- **GlobalBuildingAtlas global height RMSE:** 5.3 m (USA) against 5.5 m (ASIA and the paper as read in REST); the paper's range by continent is 1.5 m to 8.9 m.
- **GlobalBuildingAtlas access:** the ASIA report opened the Ulaanbaatar tile by rsync from the mediaTUM record's representative share, using the password printed on that record; the REST run did not use it and measured one Europe tile from an anonymous Zenodo re-tiling instead (Berlin Mitte).
- **3D-GloBFP:** the USA report calls it worse than Overture's measured heights without a number; REST measured +8.29 m, +6.41 m, +0.43 m and +43.69 m median differences in four boxes.
- **Microsoft licence:** CDLA Permissive 2.0 on Microsoft's page (USA, REST) against ODbL on Overture's attribution page (REST).
- **Overture NYC and São Paulo heights:** equal to the city layers because they are OSM imports (USA, AMERICAS, REST section 9.5), so those cities show no gain.
- **Wellington footprints:** an earlier reading of `approx_hei` as a height was corrected in REST: it is a rooftop elevation above sea level, so Wellington adds no height.
