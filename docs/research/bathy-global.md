# Global, Arctic and Southern Ocean bathymetry, and worldwide lakes, reservoirs and rivers
Run date 2026-11-13 (board item i1315). Research only. Method: four area sub-agents (global ocean grids; measured archives, shallow-water and commercial sources; Arctic and Southern Ocean; lakes, reservoirs and rivers), each told to cite only pages or API answers fetched with a 2xx answer in the run; User-Agent `scenic-roads/0.1 (personal offline map)`; files were fetched whole only where a row names what was read from them (HEAD requests for other sizes). Figures are the publishers' own, not recomputed. Regional and national hydrographic offices are left to #1316-#1322 (pointers in section 7). "Measured" below means the GEBCO TID codes 10-17 (direct soundings, lidar, optical); codes 40-48 are predicted, interpolated or derived, 70-72 unknown.

## 1. Headline

- Best global grid: GEBCO_2026 (15 arc-second, 43200 x 86400, published April 2026, public domain per gebco.net, Open Government Licence v3 per the CEDA licence file; zips on CEDA, sub-ice and ice-surface variants). It is the only global grid that ships a per-cell Type Identifier (TID) grid beside the elevations (netCDF zip 94 MB, 3.5 GB unpacked, 1 byte per cell; GeoTIFF in 8 tiles of 90 x 90 degrees), so the map can draw measured cells (TID 10-17) differently from predicted ones (40-48). Publisher figure: 28.7% of the ocean floor mapped to modern standards in 2026 (27.3% in 2025, 26.1% in 2024). The ocean part is built on SRTM15+ V2.8 (satellite-altimetry-predicted) with Seabed 2030 multibeam blended in.
- Finer where multibeam exists: GMRT v4.5.0 (June 2026) serves ~100 m multibeam cells, and its `layer=topo-mask` returns only measured cells (NaN elsewhere); publisher: 1,644 surveys = 12.41% of the global ocean; CC BY 4.0 on the terms page; no rate limit or caching clause is stated. Survey footprints: `gmrt_swath_polygons.zip` (58 MB).
- Where measured-vs-predicted masks live: GEBCO TID (all codes quoted in 2.1); IBCAO v5.2 TID and SID at 100 m; IBCSO v2 TID, RID and a coverage geopackage; GMRT topo-mask; BedMachine `source`/`dataid`; 3D-LAKES L1 (altimetry-derived) vs L2 (interpolated). SRTM15+ and ETOPO 2022 carry only a source-file or source-dataset id (no measured/predicted class); ETOPO 2022's ocean is mostly GEBCO 2022, so it is the weaker choice.
- Open for a personal offline cache (licence verified): GEBCO, ETOPO 2022 ("all private, academic or commercial purposes"), GMRT (CC BY 4.0; the terms say browse freely but do not circulate or publish without citation), NCEI multibeam and trackline archives ("free to the public with no restrictions"), NCEI/DCDB crowdsourced bathymetry (public release intended under CC0), IBCAO v5.2 (OGL v3), IBCSO v2 (CC BY 4.0), Bedmap3 (CC BY 4.0), RTopo-2.0.4 (CC BY 4.0), Allen Coral Atlas bathymetry (CC BY 4.0, reefs shallower than 20 m, 30N-30S), GLOBathy (data CC0), HydroLAKES (CC BY 4.0), the Victoria / Albert / Edward / George soundings (CC0), Kartverket's Norwegian seabed DTMs (CC BY 4.0). Not allowed or no terms for a derivative cache: Navionics/Garmin SonarChart and Community Edits (Garmin terms forbid derivative works), C-MAP, TCarta, EOMAP, Olex (all proprietary, no open download); OpenSeaMap depth has no download endpoint found. Unclear: SIO products (SRTM15+ V2.7 and SYNBATH have no stated licence; the 1-minute SIO topo grid is free for educational, research and non-profit use only), BedMachine Antarctica and Greenland (NSIDC: free access, must cite, no licence line; Earthdata Login), ATL24 (Earthdata Login, no licence text found), MGDS/IEDA records (vary; the Antarctic and Malawi ones read are CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 US).
- Polar: the Arctic is IBCAO v5.2 (June 2026; 100/200/400 m; EPSG:3996; 100 m depth zip 12 GB), the Southern Ocean IBCSO v2 (500 m; 23.79% direct-measurement coverage at release) plus a 2025 annual release (ibcso.org says 25.4% mapped by direct measurements). Under ice: BedMachine Antarctica v4 (500 m; v3 retired 24 Feb 2026) and Greenland v7 (150 m); Bedmap3 for the whole continent. Svalbard has no Svalbard-only open sea-floor grid; Kartverket's 5 m and 50 m national seabed DTMs (CC BY 4.0) have a record bounding box to 86N and a survey-coverage layer to check.
- Lakes: no global lake product is measured. GLOBathy (1 arc-second, 1.4+ million waterbodies, CC0) and HydroLAKES (mean depth/volume only) are modelled; 3D-LAKES (510,530 lakes, ICESat-2 + Landsat, Zenodo tables CC BY 4.0) is satellite-derived. Open measured grids among the 20 largest: the Laurentian Great Lakes (NOAA NCEI, pointer), Victoria (acoustic soundings, CC0), Baikal (GFZ shapefile from sounding data; "open access", no licence named), Malawi (seismic picks, ~450 m, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 US); Great Bear and Great Slave are digitised old maps (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). For the rest (Caspian, Tanganyika, Ladoga, Balkhash, Onega, Nicaragua, Athabasca, Titicaca) nothing open and measured was verified.
- Earlier survey (RESEARCH-BATHYMETRY.md): nearly every global and polar claim was confirmed; one updated (SRTM15+ is V2.8 in GEBCO_2026, V2.7 on the SIO server); one contradicted (the GLOBathy "CC0 vs CC BY-NC" conflict does not exist in its record); details in section 8.

## 3. Notes per product

- GEBCO licence, two texts: gebco.net (https://www.gebco.net/data-products-gridded-bathymetry-data/gebco2026-grid) says "The GEBCO Grid is placed in the public domain and may be used free of charge", users free to copy, publish, distribute, transmit, adapt and commercially exploit it. The CEDA download README (https://dap.ceda.ac.uk/bodc/gebco/global/gebco_2026/00README_catalogue_and_licence.txt) says "Use of these data is covered by the following licence(s): http://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/. When using these data you must cite them correctly using the citation given on the catalogue record." For files fetched from CEDA, the CEDA licence file is the one attached to that copy, so OGL v3 (attribution, citation) governs it; both texts permit a personal offline cache. Citing per the catalogue record satisfies both.
- GEBCO: the TID grid is the best "is it real" layer found. The 28.7% figure counts cells "mapped to modern standards"; no statement ties that to TID codes, so do not equate them. Ice-surface and sub-ice variants differ only over Greenland and Antarctica (BedMachine). GEBCO's terms say the grid interpolates measured data, so its resolution "may be significantly different" from the source data. Earlier grids (2019-2025) stay available. GEBCO_2026 WMS layers went live 4 Aug 2026.
- SRTM15+: the SID is per source file, so a measured flag needs the file list join (only the V2.4 list was fetched; it carries public/private flag, agency, year). GEBCO already integrates this and offers TID, so SRTM15+ adds nothing for a measured mask.
- ETOPO 2022: in the ocean mainly GEBCO 2022; superseded in detail by GEBCO_2026.
- GMRT: topo-mask is the only global per-cell "this is real multibeam" signal and is limited to ~100 m multibeam cells. Unmasked cells are GEBCO-filled (the GridServer page still says GEBCO_08, stale against the About page's GEBCO 2026 base). Terms page gives no bulk-use rule; the 58 MB swath polygon zip is the lighter first step.
- NCEI archives: multibeam is raw sonar files, not grids; useful for footprints (layer fields incl. DOWNLOAD_URL) and for custom gridding, expensive in space (70+ TB at DCDB).
- CSB: soundings are uncalibrated and carry a platform, so treat as lower-trust measured data; the S3 listing is anonymous.
- ATL24: v3 is current; photons only reach ~40 m in clear water.
- IBCAO: the only polar product with a full per-cell TID and SID. IBCSO measured shares differ by source and date: 23.79% (PANGAEA abstract, 2022), "24%" (Bedmap3 paper), 25.4% (ibcso.org now).
- BedMachine has no TID-like grid; `source=10`/`dataid=10` mark multibeam. NASA OMG delivers only the underlying soundings, with no published fjord-level mask.
- Kartverket: no Svalbard-only grid; the `surveys` collection was queried (row 37). Mareano/NGU/IMR on Geonorge (https://kartkatalog.geonorge.no/api/search, texts "Mareano batymetri", "NGU Svalbard havbunn") return only sampling, video-station, sediment and marine-landscape records (NGU "Marine landskap" uuid 32196db3-9faa-43b8-b289-cd297f0eefe8, not read for Svalbard) and no Svalbard depth grid; Mareano's terrain models are delivered through Kartverket's records. Raw soundings (uuid 2fe7b56c-334d-4660-ac50-6fcf973a0f70) are access-controlled ("Beskyttet").
- Lakes: GLOBathy and HydroLAKES are safe for a personal cache; 3D-LAKES has a real licence conflict (Zenodo CC BY 4.0 vs paper CC BY-NC-ND 4.0), so take the Zenodo tables and treat the paper's licence as a risk for the maps. Lake-specific "measured" quality varies: Malawi is seismic picks at ~450 m, Great Bear and Great Slave digitised old maps, Victoria and the White Nile lakes the best open set.
