# 3D building data for Canada, Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean and South America

Run date 2026-11-13. Read-only research (task #1324) for Scenic Roads. Method: four sub-agents (Sonnet, medium): (1) Canada federal + west, (2) Canada east + the Overture sample for all regions, (3) Mexico, Central America, Caribbean, (4) South America. Each fetched pages and API answers (CKAN, Socrata, Opendatasoft, ArcGIS REST counts and field lists, Hub search, WFS hits, parquet range reads) with User-Agent `scenic-roads/0.1 (personal offline map)`. I merged the rows by hand into one table per region and read every merged row before writing the headline. Table columns throughout: jurisdiction/product | product, version | publisher | holds | heights or storeys: how measured, what they mean | completeness | year | format and access | licence; personal offline cache?; ODbL-mixable? | footprint/coverage-index URL | vs Overture | evidence URLs fetched. The US report (buildings-usa.md) covers Microsoft, GHSL, GBA, 3D-GloBFP, WSF3D, Esri, Cesium and Google 3D Tiles; only differences are stated here. Short URLs below are exact; query strings are given where they matter.

## 1. Headline

- **Canada is the one country in these regions with a national measured-height layer, and Overture does not carry it.** NRCan's Automatically Extracted Buildings (Optimized layer, `auto_building_opti_2`): 12,393,262 lidar-derived footprints, each with `heightmin`/`heightmax` (height above ground of the lowest/highest lidar point classed Building inside the footprint; the spec warns it can be a chimney or tree branch), OGL-Canada, one 2.4 GB GeoParquet readable by HTTP range; 249 lidar projects (2006-2026). In 12 city boxes 100 % of buildings had `heightmax`; Overture's Canadian heights come only from Microsoft ML (14.6 % Montréal centre, 26.0 % Toronto, 45.0 % Vancouver, 15-17 % rural Saskatchewan). Overlap measured against Overture (section 13.3): in a Toronto box 64.5 % of NRCan buildings fall inside an Overture footprint, and 68.9 % would add a height Overture lacks; Montréal 57.2 %, Calgary 36.0 %. NRCan's heights run higher than Overture's mostly-Microsoft ones (median ratio 1.4-1.7). Quality is mixed: many areas are rated "Poor" (leaf-on or sparse lidar) and the vertical accuracy is stated as "unknown". It covers Saskatoon, Victoria, Hamilton, Kitchener, Gatineau, Laval, Longueuil, Saint John and Moncton as well as the cities in the first round; no Nunavut project; Kamloops and Moose Jaw centre boxes returned 0 rows.
- **Canadian city/provincial layers with measured heights, open and mixable (all licence texts now read except as noted):** Toronto 3D Massing 2025 (428,184 records, MIN/MAX/AVG_HEIGHT, 96.5 % lidar-derived, OGL-Toronto) and Building Outlines (557,694, DERIVED_HEIGHT); New Brunswick GeoNB Buildings (661,348 lidar polygons 2015-2018); Calgary 3D Buildings (457,136 multipatch models; the portal's OGL and the metadata PDF's "all rights reserved" conflict); Edmonton Rooflines 2019 (384,228 with building_height) and its 3D GDB (383,329 MultiPatch buildings with BLDGHEIGHT, ROOFFORM (Gable 94.5 %), BASEELEV: procedural roofs, not per-plane); Vancouver 2009 lidar footprints (124,181 with height and roof type); Surrey (137,305 of 137,657), Burnaby, Kelowna (heights, meaning undocumented). **LoD2:** Montréal's textured CityGML LoD2 (2009, 2013, 2016, 2020; CC BY 4.0) covers only 6 of 19 boroughs (2020: 42 tiles in Outremont, Côte-des-Neiges-NDG, Ville-Marie; 2016: 73 tiles in six boroughs; Outremont 2020 counted at 3,537 buildings with measuredHeight); Ottawa has LoD1 boxes (116 neighbourhood downloads). **Footprints only or no layer:** Québec's Référentiel (3,421,090 footprints; Alt* fields are ground elevations), Ontario LIO (201,238), Nova Scotia NSTDB (19,243), Halifax (172,402; a height-source tag but no value), Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, PEI, NL, territories (Yukon 25,605). StatCan's ODB v3 (14,417,429 buildings counted; height>0 on 1,212,709 = 8.4 %, only AB 141,337 and BC 345,635, ON 703,803, NB 21,934; none in QC, MB, SK, NS, PE, NL, YT, NT) and Public Safety's Canada Structures (13.76 M, 9,156,321 with HRDEM-mean heights) are re-packagings of the above plus OSM/Microsoft; fetch the originals.
- **Mexico: no national footprint product, but two storey layers exist.** INEGI's 1:50,000 topography draws "Edificación" only outside blocks, without height (dictionary v2 read: attributes IDENTIFICADOR, TIPO, NOMBRE); the Marco Geoestadístico layer is blocks; the Inventario Nacional de Viviendas counts dwellings per block (1,596,975 urban manzanas), not buildings. Found on retry: Guadalajara municipality's cadastral WFS `gic:Construccion_2025` (1,349,854 construction-block polygons with integer `niveles`: 1 storey 717,650, 2 511,892, 3 97,427, up to 40+; licence not found) and Mexico City's Atlas de Riesgos lot layers with `niveles` (873,607 lots in 10 of 16 alcaldías, survey dates 2014 in the samples; lots, not footprints; licence not found). INEGI's "Términos de libre uso" were read (copy and commercial use allowed with credit): cache yes, ODbL-mixable yes by attribution only. Other state and city hosts (Jalisco state, Nuevo León, Puebla, Yucatán, Tijuana, Querétaro, CDMX's own portals) resolve but time out or refuse from this network. Overture's footprints there are OSM, Google and Microsoft with almost no heights (Mexico City centre 0.1 %, Oaxaca 0.03 %, Zacatecas 0).
- **Central America and the Caribbean:** the only measured heights beyond Overture are IGN France's BD TOPO for Guadeloupe (hauteur on 364,432 of 384,282), Martinique (338,324 of 378,718), Saint-Barthélemy (8,538 of 8,573) and Saint-Martin (16,067 of 16,096), Licence Ouverte 2.0, photogrammetric; and the same product for French Guiana (83,607 of 90,342). Costa Rica's IGN catalogues define a storey attribute on 3D building polygons (2016 edition CANTIDAD DE NIVELES in three classes: one, two, three or more; 2020 edition PISOS); schema only: every SNIT host refuses connections and the WFS moved to geos.snitcr.go.cr/be/IGN_5_CO/wfs (captures show no edificaciones layer). Nicaragua's INETER layer has 7,349 polygons with a `hgt` field empty on 94 % of a sample. The World Bank/GFDRR Dominica and Saint Lucia building sets are not public. Dutch Caribbean: BAG has no buildings on Bonaire; Bonaire, Saba, St Eustatius have 2023-24 DSM+DTM (CC BY 4.0). Panama, Honduras, El Salvador, Guatemala, Belize, Santo Domingo, Havana: no building layer reached.
- **South America (storeys and heights beyond Overture):** Brasília/DF `Edificação` (new): 820,048 footprints with `ed_num_pav` storeys (>0 on 818,072, max 42) and `ed_alt_aprox` metres (>0 on 815,707, max 126.46), licence not found. São Paulo 2,817,745 edificações with `qt_altura_edificacao` in metres, now known to be CC BY-SA 4.0 (ODbL-mixable no); Rio 2,295,829 with ALTURA (CC BY 4.0, 2013 imagery); Belo Horizonte 739,100 rows with DSM-minus-DTM heights (CC BY, 2015); Fortaleza 2016 (a CSV of exactly 1,000,000 rows, altura and pav; possibly truncated by an export cap); Buenos Aires "Tejido Urbano" (1,386,616 rows, altura in 2.8 m steps, CC BY 2.5 AR, CSV without geometry); Río Cuarto (86,533, altura class); Bogotá's Mapa de Referencia Construcción with `CONNPISOS` storeys on 2,371,609 of 2,386,926 polygons (programme-level CC BY 4.0 notice read); French Guiana BD TOPO. Quito publishes 3D scene services by zone (2026, I3S meshes, no attributes). No open LoD2 city model with attributes anywhere in South America. Chile (permit points with NUM_PISOS, CC BY-NC), Peru, Uruguay (MDS 2024 and a regulatory heights layer, no building heights), Bolivia, Paraguay, Venezuela, Guyana, Suriname, Falklands: nothing open with building heights reached.
- **Overture sample (22 boxes, section 13):** the only per-property height source in these regions is Microsoft ML Buildings (Canada). In Latin America heights come inside OSM records: São Paulo 93.3 %, Rio 46.8 %, Lima 8.5 %, Cusco/Mexico City/Bogotá/Buenos Aires/Santiago under 1.1 %, Guatemala City 0.006 %. Google Open Buildings footprints are present in Mexico and all of Latin America (e.g. Guatemala City 30,808 of 53,453), Esri Community Maps in Bogotá (67,246), City of Vancouver in Vancouver (227 in the box). Google, Esri and Zenodo carry no height.
- **What each valuable source would actually add (section 13.3, one box each, centroid-in-footprint join):** São Paulo almost nothing (97.3 % of matched buildings already have an Overture height, all from OSM and equal to GeoSampa's `qt_altura_edificacao` to 0.00 m in 98.7 % of pairs: Overture's OSM is an import of it); Rio adds footprints (24.0 % unmatched) and heights on 65.4 % of its buildings; NRCan adds a height to 36-69 % of its buildings (Toronto 68.9 %, Montréal 57.2 %, Calgary 36.0 %); Toronto massing adds a height to 65.3 % (Overture has a Microsoft ML or no height); Bogotá adds storeys to 92.9 % (Overture num_floors on 6.0 %, height on 0.6 %).
- **Google Open Buildings v3 covers Mexico, the countries of Central America and the Caribbean and all South America as listed on its page (not Canada, Aruba, Anguilla, Guadeloupe, Martinique); the 2.5D Temporal height raster (Sentinel-2, ~4 m effective, MAE 1.5 m measured only in North America, Europe and Japan) covers the same countries.** Licence CC BY 4.0 or ODbL, user's choice (page text read): cache yes, ODbL-mixable yes. The footprints are already in Overture (confirmed by the sample); the 2.5D heights are not (no Google per-property height entry in 22 boxes) and are worse than any measured height above, a fallback where Overture has no height.
- **Open for a personal offline cache and ODbL-mixable (licence text read):** NRCan AEB and ODB (OGL-Canada), Toronto, Vancouver, Burnaby, Kelowna, Victoria, Regina, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Ottawa (Wayback), Yukon (Wayback), Edmonton (all OGL-family, attribution only), Montréal 3D, Québec Référentiel (CC BY 4.0), Ontario LIO, New Brunswick, Surrey, Rio (CC BY 4.0), Belo Horizonte and Fortaleza (CKAN cc-by), Uruguay's open-data licence (merging with third-party data allowed), INEGI's libre uso, BD TOPO (Licence Ouverte 2.0), Google Open Buildings (CC BY / ODbL). Bogotá: a 2025 city notice puts the Mapa de Referencia under CC BY 4.0 (layer item itself empty). **Cache yes, ODbL-mixable no:** São Paulo GeoSampa (CC BY-SA 4.0; CC's compatibility page lists no ODbL), Valle del Cauca R2 (CC BY-SA), Chile's INE permit points (CC BY-NC). **Unclear:** Calgary (OGL on the portal, "all rights reserved" in the metadata PDF), Buenos Aires (CC BY 2.5 AR: "cannot sublicense" clause), Guadalajara and CDMX storey layers (no licence found), Brasília (none found), Jamaica NLA (none), Nicaragua ("all rights reserved" note), Toronto's disclaimers aside. **No cache:** Medellín's restricted working copy (authorised users only), GFDRR Caribbean labels (not public), BC Assessment inventory (BC government only), Trinidad lidar (non-commercial).
- **Unverified** (section 14): the Vancouver dataset behind Overture's 14,209-record "City of Vancouver" source (unlisted layer versioned 2025-09-10), the Calgary licence conflict, Costa Rica's data, the Mexican state and city portals, Medellín's and Cali's city layers, Lima, Chile's cadastre and census points, Argentine provincial cadastres (IDERA's catalogue is broken), PE3D/Recife, Bogotá's own portal licence, Canadian Lethbridge/Airdrie licences.

## 2. Evidence caveats

- Every cell rests on a page or API answer fetched in this run; items reached only by search pointers are in the "nothing better found" tables or Unverified. The first round worked to a 5 MB per-request cap; the PM lifted it, and a second round of four agents re-read the cells the cap had left open (ODB zips, Edmonton 3D GDB, Montréal 2D and LoD2 tile counts, RefBati GPKG, Toronto's full dbf, Buenos Aires and Fortaleza CSVs), read the licence pages, retried Mexico and the South American blanks and measured overlap with Overture. Rows are updated in place; cells that still say unread name the reason.
- Overture ingestion of each portal layer is not tested per layer; section 13 gives what sources appear in 22 boxes. "Probably not inside Overture" means the sources named in the sample and on Overture's attribution/buildings pages do not include it.
- Counts from ArcGIS/Socrata/WFS are the publisher's live answers on 2026-11-13.

## 11. Notes per jurisdiction

- **NRCan AEB:** two feature classes: "by acquisition source" (duplicates where sources overlap) and "Optimized" (one footprint per building, source in md_id_src). 249 per-project GPKG zips (listing re-read): QC 93, ON 35, AB 25, MB 25, BC 16, SK 13, YT 12, NB 11, NS 10, NL 5, NT 1, NU 1, PE 1. Large cities checked against the KMZ and by rows in a 3 km centre box of the optimized parquet: Saskatoon 5,068, Victoria 3,744, Hamilton 5,394, Kitchener 7,619, Gatineau 5,872, Laval 5,314, Longueuil 3,842, Saint John NB 2,338, Moncton 3,301 are covered; Kamloops and Moose Jaw returned 0 rows (probe only, not citywide). The download-telecharger copy of the KMZ returned 403 (not bypassed); the same file was read from ftp.maps.canada.ca. West projects include Calgary East/West 2020, Edmonton 2018, Lethbridge 2019, Medicine Hat, Red Deer, Airdrie, Fort McMurray, Grande Prairie, Lower Mainland 2016, Okanagan 2018, Kootenay, Vancouver Island, VILLE_VANCOUVER/SURREY/BURNABY/NANAIMO (city-supplied), Winnipeg 2019, Red River 2020, VILLE_REGINA, Yellowknife UTM11 2021, Whitehorse 2019. Where lidar is "Poor" (leaf-on, low density) heightmax may include trees. Box medians of heightmax: Toronto 10.9 m, Montréal 12.8, Ottawa 9.2, Québec 9.2, Halifax 8.4, Fredericton 6.5, Moncton 6.7, St. John's 6.9, Charlottetown 6.7, Hamilton 6.8, London 7.1, Sherbrooke 7.2. The parquet is on ftp.maps.canada.ca (2,415,243,127 bytes, footer 4.1 MB); the download-telecharger host returned 403 on HEAD for the same file.
- **Canada height meaning:** Calgary gives roof top and ground min/max, so eave versus roof can be chosen; Edmonton gives building_height directly; Vancouver 2009 hgt_agl = topelev minus baseelev; Toronto massing heights are above ground; GeoNB gives two elevations. Surrey/Burnaby/Kelowna fields have no description. BC Assessment's residential inventory (storeys) is EULA-restricted to BC government and post-secondary institutions: not usable.
- **Québec:** the Référentiel holds NC-0 raw and NC-1 validated geometries; municipal footprints (Québec City, Laval with TYPE_RELEVE incl. Lidar, Sherbrooke partial, Saguenay, Longueuil) have no height field. Montréal's LoD2 sizes make a city-wide cache large (several zips of 1.3-2.5 GB per borough group).
- **Calgary:** Socrata reads need curl (urllib failed the TLS chain); attachments are signed S3 redirects.
- **Mexico:** datos.gob.mx CKAN queries (edificaciones 1 hit, huellas de edificios 27 all INDAABIN federal property lists, catastro 2, altura edificios 0, modelo 3D 2) show no footprint or height dataset. ISPRS Archives XLVIII-3/W4-2025 (TU Delft, CC BY 4.0) says Mexico's national topographic and elevation datasets lack individual footprints; it builds footprints from DSMs for central CDMX (about 30 % of small buildings missed); no data release seen. CDMX SEDUVI land use is points per predio.
- **BD TOPO overseas:** same WFS also answers for Réunion (445,994 with height in the bbox) and Guyane; field list read from a sample feature: cleabs, nature, usage_1, usage_2, nombre_d_etages, hauteur, altitude_minimale_sol, altitude_minimale_toit, altitude_maximale_toit, origine_du_batiment, identifiants_rnb, date_modification.
- **Brazil:** Belo Horizonte's two height fields are estimates from a 2015 DEM/DTM. Rio's pgeo3 server has a 2019 lidar terrain service but no building product was read. No LoD2 anywhere. Buenos Aires: the portal also lists parcelas, area-edificable-volumen and "Ciudad 3D" (a viewer; no downloadable data per the pages read); IGN Argentina's WFS layer ign:areas_de_asentamientos_y_edificios_020105 (3,350 features) has only gid, entidad, objeto, geom and names.
- **Colombia:** the arcgis.com search found nothing for Medellín or Cali polygons with pisos; Valle del Cauca R1/R2 tables exist for other departments (Risaralda: Pereira, Dosquebradas, Virginia) with pisos per predio but no geometry.
- **Storeys instead of heights:** Bogotá (CONNPISOS), Costa Rica (PISOS, schema only), Valle del Cauca (pisos, table), Fortaleza (pav), Belo Horizonte (storeys estimable from the moda height), Toronto none, ODB "floors" where the provider had it, BD TOPO `nombre_d_etages` (Guadeloupe 34.8 %, Martinique 9 rows). The map's floors-times-storey-height rule (docs/buildings3d.md §2.3) can use them.

## 15. Accidental

- NRCan AEB's GeoParquet allows bbox range reads (1,242 row groups of ~10 k rows), so a per-tile fetch needs only a few MB; NRCan counts 13.6 M footprints including duplicates by acquisition source (2.58 M added with 61 projects).
- Canada Structures' spec reveals the HRDEM-mean height method (DSM minus DTM) for Population Centres only.
- NRCan's GeoAI series (deep-learning footprints) exists beside AEB; Yukon's footprint record (published 2026-03-09) is new against Overture's address-only Yukon entry; ODB's provider appendix lists some custom (non-OGL) licences.
- BD TOPO's WFS on data.geopf.fr is open to anonymous bbox/`hauteur` queries and also serves Réunion and Guyane.
- INEGI's CNIT 2024 is now one national dataset (17.27 M features), not sheet-based.
- Trinidad and Tobago's lidar DTM item carries "not for commercial use" text.
- datos.gob.mx CKAN works; many other Mexican and Central American government hosts refused connections from this network.
- Bogotá's Construcción layer answers plain ArcGIS REST, a cheap 2.37 M-polygon storeys source.
- The Overture "City of Vancouver" source is a 2025-09-10 layer with float heights that the Vancouver open-data catalogue does not list.
- Overture's OSM heights in São Paulo equal GeoSampa's heights to 0.00 m on 98.7 % of buildings (an import in all but name).
- Fortaleza's CSV has exactly 1,000,000 rows, a likely export cap.
- Brasília's ArcGIS server (geoservicos.ide.df.gov.br) holds an 820,048-building layer with storeys and heights that the geoportal page does not expose.
- The Wayback copy of Yukon's and Ottawa's licence pages, and Kelowna's PDF, are the only readable texts (live hosts block scripted clients; not bypassed). Scratch folders of two agents were deleted from outside mid-run.
- Valle del Cauca R1/R2 tables exist for several Colombian departments (storeys per predio, no geometry).
