## 3. Notes per jurisdiction

#### Germany
Best open sea-floor sources, in order of use for a 3D map: BSH 50 m INSPIRE grid (row 6; DL-DE-BY-2.0, one 77 MB zip for both seas, WCS for subsets) [A3][A7]; BAW EasyGSH-DB 10 m annual grids (row 12), SMMS 1960 to 2020 (row 13) and TrilaWatt (row 14) for the German Bight and Wadden Sea including the tidal flats, but interpolated and with licence text to clarify [A16][A17][A20][A21]; EMODnet HR cells for Kiel, Flensburg, Lübeck bays, Bodden East, Jade, Ems, Weser, Elbe (row 11, 85 BSH polygons in the box) [A13]. The BSH 50 m grid has no cell-level source layer; use SVDB viewer and the per-survey single-beam sets (rows 8 and 9) as coverage evidence [A10][A12].

The BSH results page says the 50 m grids are "free of charge according to GeoZG", "1 m ... on an FTP server", fee possible [A1]. The current INSPIRE record gives DL-DE-BY-2.0 and the older 2013 record GeoNutzV with NHN datum [A3][A9]. The vertical datum of the 2026 grid is not written in the INSPIRE metadata or WCS capabilities [A4][A8].

Rivers and estuaries: the Elbe is the best case: DGM-W Unter-/Außenelbe 2022 at 1 m with a source map (row 15) and BfG's inland Elbe/Lower Havel 1 m model on Zenodo CC BY 4.0 (row 16), 12.7 GB [A22][A25]. WSV's INSPIRE DGM-W service covers only selected federal waterways (row 17), bbox over the Elbe line in the WMS layer [A26][A27]. Weser: HIWEST historical DTMs (row 18) [A28]. Trave and Lübeck Bay: row 19 [A29]. No DGM-W was found for Rhine, Main, Mosel, Neckar, Ems, Danube Germany in govdata (search "Rhein", "Gewässersohle", "DGM-W" gave only the Elbe items) [A52].

Länder: Schleswig-Holstein offers 1995 to 2005 Wadden Sea height-class polygons (row 20) [A31]; Brandenburg lake depth-band polygons (row 23) [A33]; Hamburg a merged port surface referencing the DGM-W 2016 (row 21) [A32]. Nothing found for NLWKN, Bremen, HPA, M-V coast, Bavarian lakes, LUBW, Talsperren (rows 22, 25).

Lakes: Bodensee was re-surveyed 2013 to 2015 (multibeam > 5 m depth plus bathymetric lidar), maximum depth 251.14 m below mean level versus 253.55 m in 1990; only a 1:75,000 PDF depth map is published, though the IGKB said data would be made public [A34][A35][A36].

#### Denmark and the Faroes
DDM 50 m v2.0 (row 26) is the one national grid: free incl. commercial use, not for navigation, datum MSL (DKMSL 2022), per-cell source and year layers, GeoTIFF via a weblink and a token-gated WMS [A37][A38][A39][A40][A41]. Its downloads can be closed by GST at any time, but a downloaded copy stays usable on the old terms [A38]. Finer Danish data: Danish Wadden Sea HR cells from Deltares in EMODnet (328 polygons) [A13][A14]. No Danish lake bathymetry found open.

Faroes: FODM50_2026 (row 30) is open, annual, with a source-class layer; 17.4 M of 107.4 M cells measured [A42][A43][A44]. Jarðfeingi multibeam cells in EMODnet (8 polygons) [A13][A45].

#### Iceland
Open: MFRI's 22 gridded multibeam areas, 10 to 20 m, GeoTIFF and text, with a citation line only [A46][A47]. #### Baltic-wide and Nordic Seas
- BSBD is not a measured product: BSHC itself calls it "modeled data" and puts no per-country source resolution on the page; the only quality information is the "Density overview / Source resolution" GeoTIFF [B1][B3]. The licence is CC BY 3.0 with a no-navigation restriction, allowing a personal offline cache [B1]. For all practical purposes EMODnet (global report) supersedes BSBD, and the working group was disbanded in its favour [B1][B4].

- EMODnet HR areas: counted 359 polygons in lat 54..71, lon 4..32 [B8]. - Olex and MAREANO: see NONGOV and row 5.

#### Norway
- Best source: Kartverket via Geonorge. The national 50 m terrain model (rows 35-36) is CC BY 4.0 with 1,706 download cells (tile index = the Geonorge area codelist) in XYZ and TIFF [B11][B20]; the 25 m and 5 m models only exist where MAREANO mapped (offshore), because "Dybdedata med høyere oppløsning enn 50m x 50m tilknyttet Norges territorialfarvann er gradert informasjon" [B12][B13]. This confirms the classification rule: nothing finer than 50 m is published inside the 12 nm territorial sea; fine coastal and fjord data exist only on application (row 40) or in the few unclassified sets (NGU Oslofjord 1 m; Søre Sunnmøre 5 m) [B14][B16][B19].
- Vertical datum per the product specification: chart datum (K0) nearshore, mean sea level offshore [B24]. Mixed datums in one model: convert before merging. The chart-database vector layer (row 39) is referenced to sjøkartnull [B15].
- Coverage layer: the WMS at survey.kartverket.no offers `bathy_by_year` and `bathy_by_resolution`; the OGC API for "Dybdedata - dekning" is advertised but its host returned HTTP 500 in this run [B17][B25]. Polygons carry a quality class 1-4 [B17].
- Lakes: NVE's Innsjødatabase has 530 surveyed lakes with 27,886 depth contours and 28,512 depth points in an open ArcGIS service (NLOD) [B27][B26].
