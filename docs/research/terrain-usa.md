# Highest-resolution terrain data for the USA: 3DEP, every state, DC, territories, non-governmental

Run date 2026-11-12. Read-only research for Scenic Roads (personal offline map). Method: every claim carries the URL of a page or API answer fetched and read in this run (by me or by the sub-agents I briefed). Search-result snippets alone were not used. Requests used the User-Agent `scenic-roads/0.1 (personal offline map)`. No bulk downloads (tiny probes and metadata only; see "Conduct notes").

URL keys used in the tables (full URL, defined once):
- IDX8 = https://index.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevationIndex/MapServer/8 (3DEP "Lidar Point Cloud" work-unit index, 2,870 records, fetched 2026-11-12)
- IDX1 = https://index.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevationIndex/MapServer/1 (3DEP 1 m DEM footprint)
- USIEI = https://coast.noaa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USInteragencyElevationInventory/USIEIv2/MapServer/2 (NOAA/USGS US Interagency Elevation Inventory, lidar layer, includes planned and in-progress projects)
- NOAA-S3 = https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/ (NOAA Digital Coast lidar bucket, anonymous)
- TNM-S3 = https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/ (USGS National Map staged products, anonymous)
- TRACKER = https://3dntmtracker-api.nationalmap.gov/api/projectGeometry (USGS 3DNTM acquisition tracker, found via https://www.sco.wisc.edu/2026/10/09/check-the-status-of-3dep-acquisition-projects-with-3dntm/)

Verdict codes: BETTER = state/other data finer, denser, newer, or covering 3DEP gaps; MIRROR = state serves the same projects 3DEP indexes (state may add format or convenience); 3DEP-BEST = nothing better found. "+" after MIRROR means a convenience product (seamless COG, county mosaic). Percent figures are shares of the Census TIGERweb state polygon (includes water) computed by me from IDX8 and IDX1; they were computed at the start of the run and recomputed from a fresh pull of the live index at 16:13 the same day (2,870 work units, identical QL/category tallies, identical per-state shares), see section 3.

## 1. Headline

- 3DEP is the floor everywhere and the ceiling in about 36 of 56 jurisdictions. State data beats it, at least locally, in about 20: substantially in CT, DC, NC, OR, TX, WA, GU, AS; locally in AK, CA, FL, HI, IN, MP, MT, ND (cities), NY (NYC), UT, VA (Richmond), WI (Milwaukee Co).
- Biggest gaps/uplifts: North Carolina (3DEP index has only 2001-2005 3 m legacy over the Piedmont; state/NOAA hold 2024 QL1 for 19 counties), Connecticut (2023 statewide QL1, 3DEP shows it for 34%), DC (2024 QL1, CC0), Texas (2021-2025 projects not in the index, CC0 S3), Washington (16 QL1 "Refresh" blocks 10-26 pts/m2, 1.5 ft DEM), Oregon (DOGAMI 3 ft mosaic ~99% of state vs 3DEP 70% 1 m), American Samoa and Guam (NOAA 2020-22 topobathy is the only modern data), NYC 2021 (12 ppsm, 2 ft DTM).
- 3DEP index gaps in AZ, UT, NV (and parts of NM, ID, MT) are mostly tribal land (data collected but withheld by USGS policy) and DoD ranges, not "never flown": see section 3.
- Non-governmental sources never beat 3DEP in CONUS, Hawaii or Puerto Rico; they are 3DEP re-hosts, research footprints, or licence-restricted (section 5). Only independent gap-fill: ArcticDEM 2 m DSM for Alaska (CC BY 4.0).
- Licence for a personal offline map: 3DEP and the NOAA/USGS copies are public domain / no restrictions; most states are CC0, public domain or "cite us". Restricted: Illinois ISGS (all rights reserved wording), Minnesota DNR (no commercial resale of the data "in its entirety"), SEMCOG (copyright notice), LA County LARIAC (NDA), Knox County TN and Georgia Mountains RC (purchase / restricted).

## 3. National: USGS 3DEP

Everything in this section was read by me in this run unless a sub-agent is named. USGS pages: https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program , https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/topographic-data-quality-levels-qls , https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services , https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/3dep-spatial-metadata , https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/fy26-status-3dep-quality-data , https://www.usgs.gov/ngp-standards-and-specifications/lidar-base-specification-online (all fetched with curl, HTTP 200).

- National status. "This map of the United States and its territories shows the completion of the first-ever national baseline of consistent high-resolution elevation data - both bare earth and 3D point clouds - including baseline and next generation coverage as of September 30, 2026." Baseline = "Quality level 2 or better lidar data (IfSAR in AK)" as defined in "3DEP Lidar Base Specification v1.2 or later and the 1-Meter Digital Elevation Model Specification, TM-11B7"; footnote "Some areas may be restricted"; sources WESM and USIEI (July 2026) [https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/fy26-status-3dep-quality-data]. The USGS gives no single percent-complete figure on these pages; the per-state shares below are mine, computed from the index. Data quality note: "Most of the data collected in 2014 and later meet 3DEP specifications for quality level 2 nominal pulse spacing and vertical accuracy, and data collected prior to 2014 often do not" [https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services]. Latest Lidar Base Specification: "2025 rev. A, was released on June 10, 2025" [https://www.usgs.gov/ngp-standards-and-specifications/lidar-base-specification-online].
- WESM = the index I used. "Work unit Extent Spatial Metadata (WESM)... contains information about what Lidar Point Cloud (LPC) and source DEMs (also known as Original Product Resolution (OPR) DEMs) work units have been processed and made available to the public... A new WESM is written almost daily"; offered as GeoPackage and CSV; status per product (LPC, source DEMs, 1 m DEMs, seamless) is "meets", "meets with variance", "does not meet", with a temporary "expected to meet" [https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/3dep-spatial-metadata]. My IDX8 pull is the same data through the TNM ArcGIS REST service (below).

- Programme and products. The 3DEP index service: DEM products at 2 arc-second (Alaska only), 1 arc-second, 1/3 arc-second, 1/9 arc-second, 5 m (Alaska only) and 1 m; 1/3 and 1 arc-second staged as 1x1 degree tiles (ArcGrid, GridFloat, IMG), 1/9 arc-second as 15 min tiles (IMG), 1 m as 10,000 x 10,000 m tiles; elevation source data = lidar point clouds (LAS) and, in Alaska only, IfSAR DSM/ORI; topobathy products for limited river areas [https://index.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevationIndex/MapServer?f=json]. Index "Data refreshed weekly" (same URL).
- Index size and fields. Lidar work-unit layer: 2,870 records with ql, spec, collect_start/end, dem_gsd_meters, horiz/vert CRS, geoid, per-product publication category ("Meets", "Does not meet", "Pending publication") and metadata links [IDX8 fields via https://index.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevationIndex/MapServer/8?f=json]. My tally of the 2,870: QL2 1,166; QL1 798; "Other" (pre-spec) 657; QL3 240; QL0 9; lpc "Meets" 1,627, "Meets with variance" 238, "Does not meet" 946, "Expected to meet" 55; onemeter "Meets" 1,622, "Pending publication" 58 [query https://index.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevationIndex/MapServer/8/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=ql,lpc_category,onemeter_category&f=geojson].
