# Research reports: data sources

Reports from the data-source research of 12–14 November 2026 (board tasks #1313–#1328), kept here
because they are small and could not be made again as they are: publishers' pages, indexes and
licence texts change, and several hosts answered only through archive copies.

Each report was written by research agents from pages, API answers and files fetched in that run;
every claim cites the URL it came from, and each says what was measured from opened files and what
is described from pages only. They quote publishers' text verbatim, so the spelling guard does not
scan this folder. Mentions of `RESEARCH-BATHYMETRY` and `RESEARCH-TERRAIN` without a region are of
earlier drafts that these replaced.

| Subject | Index | Regional reports |
|---|---|---|
| Terrain (elevation models, lidar) | `terrain-index.md` | `terrain-usa.md`, `terrain-americas.md`, `terrain-europe-w.md`, `terrain-europe-e.md`, `terrain-asia.md`, `terrain-rest.md` |
| Bathymetry (sea, lake and river depths) | `bathy-index.md` | `bathy-global.md`, `bathy-namerica.md`, `bathy-samerica.md`, `bathy-europe-w.md`, `bathy-europe-e.md`, `bathy-africa-me.md`, `bathy-asia.md`, `bathy-oceania.md` |
| 3D buildings (heights, storeys, models) | `buildings-index.md` | `buildings-usa.md`, `buildings-americas.md`, `buildings-europe-w.md`, `buildings-europe-e.md`, `buildings-asia.md`, `buildings-rest.md` |

What has been done with them: the terrain reports feed the declared terrain sources
(`producers/extents/terrain/`, docs/inputs.md §6.11). The bathymetry and buildings reports are not
yet used by the build. The owner's decisions on the questions the indexes ask are written at the
top of each index's questions section.
