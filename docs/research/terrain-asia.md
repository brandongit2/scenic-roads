# Highest-resolution terrain data for Asia and the Middle East (excluding Turkey, the Caucasus, Cyprus, Russia)
Run date 2026-11-13 (board item i1313). Research only. Method: four area sub-agents (East Asia; South-East Asia; South and Central Asia; Middle East), each told to cite only pages or API answers fetched with a 2xx answer in the run; User-Agent `scenic-roads/0.1 (personal offline map)`; no data downloads. Global products (Copernicus GLO-30, AW3D30, FABDEM etc.) are described in terrain-europe-w.md section 4 and not repeated. Verdicts: NATIONAL-OPEN, SUBNATIONAL-BETTER, RESTRICTED (finer data exists but is paid, request-only or licence-blocked), GLOBAL-ONLY. Sections 2-7 are grouped per area (x.1 East Asia, x.2 South-East Asia, x.3 South and Central Asia, x.4 Middle East). A second pass (four more sub-agents) closed gaps listed by the PM; its additions are marked 'Second pass'. Mapterhorn's source catalogue has no source in South Asia, South-East Asia or the Middle East beyond its 10 m ASTER Israel layer; it excludes Hong Kong (licence revocable at any time).
## 1. Headline

- Japan is the only country with open data far finer than the globals. GSI's DEM10B covers the whole country; DEM5A/1A (lidar) cover parts (bulk download needs registration, XYZ tiles are anonymous; footprints are anonymous GeoJSON tile layers on maps.gsi.go.jp, section 3.1.1). Prefectures beat GSI and are open: Tokyo (16+ pts/m2, 0.25 m DEM, any use), Shizuoka, Hyogo (0.5 m), Kanagawa, Hiroshima (DoboX grids), Gifu (0.5 m, opened 2025-09), Wakayama, Nagasaki, Miyagi, Yamagata, Nagano, Ehime, Kochi, Tochigi, Kyoto, Toyama, Tottori and the Forestry Agency's sets, indexed by the G-spatial information centre CKAN; the other prefectures and the cities checked share one "nothing finer found" row.
- Everywhere else no open national DTM beats the global 30 m products. Open but coarse: Taiwan 20 m (its 1 m lidar DTM is classed official-internal, not open), Hong Kong 5 m (surface-like). Finer but closed, sold or request-only: South Korea (5 m DEM free but behind an NGII login; 1 m lidar urban only), Hong Kong CEDD lidar (order-only, project-only use), Malaysia 10 m (sold), Indonesia DEMNAS about 8 m (terms not stated), Philippines 1 m (request-only; NAMRIA sells IfSAR/lidar), India (30 m or coarser CartoDEM free; finer priced or cleared), Saudi Arabia (3/5/10 m DTM for the Kingdom, city DEMs 0.5 m, permit-gated), Bahrain (1 m DTM, BD 50/km2), Iran (sheet-priced), Dubai (DTM request service), Sri Lanka (lidar DEMs on customer request), Nepal (1 m programme, no release found).
- China, Vietnam, India, Indonesia, South Korea, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and others restrict geodata by law (sections 3.x name each statute with its URL); China classes DEM/DSM better than 15 m height accuracy over more than 25 km2 as Secret, so use only global products there.
- GLOBAL-ONLY: Singapore (SLA holds lidar, publishes no elevation product), Brunei, Timor-Leste, Laos, Myanmar, Cambodia (Angkor lidar unusable), Vietnam, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Afghanistan, the five Central Asian states (OpenTopography has CC BY 4.0 Pleiades patches for Almaty and Bishkek), Mongolia, Macau, North Korea, and in the Middle East Israel (Mapterhorn's 10 m ASTER layer only), Jordan, Lebanon, Oman, Kuwait, Iraq, Syria, Yemen, Qatar, Abu Dhabi.
- What stays unverified (section 5): the licence terms of most Japanese prefecture sets, the Iran WMS, Indonesian and Philippine reuse terms, Thai/Sri Lankan/Nepali access paths, and unreachable Chinese, Mongolian, Macau and Israeli hosts.
