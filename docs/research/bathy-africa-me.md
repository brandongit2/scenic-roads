## 3. Notes per jurisdiction

**Mediterranean south and east, Red Sea, Jordan**

- **EMODnet reach (task request):** WFS capabilities (https://ows.emodnet-bathymetry.eu/wfs?service=WFS&request=GetCapabilities, 200) give the bathymetry tile layer a box of -70.5,11 to 43,90; the Red Sea (lat 12-30, lon 32-43) is inside, the Gulf of Aden only west of 43E. But the release-2024 `source_references` there are `Coastal_heights_2024` (3,656) and `GEBCO2024` (77) and one CNR-ISMAR CDI: EMODnet adds no measured Red Sea data. In the Mediterranean south/east it carries real Israel NBS and Nile Delta composites and many CDI surveys. Method note: WFS 2.0 with urn CRS and the layer's CQL BBOX both use lat,lon axis order (my first runs with lon,lat order returned Greek and Croatian sources, discarded).
- **Saudi RSDE licence conflict** (row 2): Zenodo metadata CC BY 4.0 vs TIFF xml CC BY-NC 4.0; unresolved. Preprint (ESSD, discussion since 8 May 2026) is the best technical description: LAT, 40 m EM 304 (49,284.9 km2), 5 m T50-P (133.8 km2); also says GEBCO's 15 arc-second is "insufficient".
- **Israel:** open measured data only through the Mendeley EEZ grid (CC BY-NC 3.0, 200 m) and EMODnet composites; GSI/IOLR hosts mostly 403 or unreachable. Terrain report names Survey of Israel DTM behind gov.il.
- **Egypt:** ENHD NIOHC-24 report fetched: no data release statement; terrain report says Egyptian Survey Authority shows no product. Legal limits on depth data: none found for Egypt/Morocco in the pages read; Algeria "restrictive data-sharing policies" per MTS Journal abstract (row 14). Saudi: terrain report names GEOSA permit conditions covering hydrographic marine survey.
- **Morocco:** DHOC runs the national hydrographic service (name not confirmed on a fetched page); the 2024 IHO report mentions no bathymetry data release.
- **Jordan/Dead Sea:** the Hydro International page confirms the multibeam campaign and its sound-speed trouble.
- **Allen Coral Atlas** covers Red Sea reefs (GLOBAL row 19 box 30N-30S) and is the likely best shallow set for Egypt/Sudan/Eritrea/Saudi/Yemen reefs.

**Gulf states, Iran, Iraq, Yemen**

- Saudi Arabia: IHO RSAHC10 report slide gives 60 % of the Arabian Gulf surveyed, 3 projects with km2, finish planned 2026, C-55 60/40/0; products are charts supplied "on request" (URL row 25). Live geosa.gov.sa answers "Request Rejected" to curl; I went to the Wayback copy rather than retry. Legal limits: terrain report names the permit conditions for hydrographic work; I did not fetch them.
- UAE: Dubai (DMSD) is an IHO member and ENC producer; Abu Dhabi's depth layers (EOMAP) are not in its open ADSDI server; its open marine layers are habitats only (row 27). No federal hydrographic office page read.
- Bahrain is the only Gulf state with a published price list for depth data (85 fils per reading, min BD 50), so a bulk personal cache is not an intended use (row 32).
- Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain submitted no national report at RSAHC10 (Nov 2024, Jeddah); Iraq was not yet a full member; Oman reports one operational survey vessel.
- Oman: ONHO archives all surveys including commercial ones; nothing is published. Surveys by the Royal Navy are under a defence ministry.
- Iran: charting by PMO; INCOD catalog offline per ODIS; hosts unreachable. No legal text read for hydrographic restrictions.
- Regional: ROPME has no bathymetry product found. The Persian Gulf bathymetry that exists openly is GEBCO's (Gulf contributions to GEBCO are chart-derived, see row 43; GEBCO_2014 had known deep-water artifacts in the shallow Gulf per a poster, search only).
- The Gulf is shallow and turbid: the Abu Dhabi benchmark found the seabed "could not be read beyond 11 m" and RMSE 1.1 to 1.7 m, so SDB for the Gulf is rough.

**West, Central and Southern Africa**

- South Africa: the best open set is the 100 m SANBI grid (row 57), non-commercial and share-alike; its input multibeam is not redistributable at native resolution (DataCite abstract). SANHO publishes ENCs and charts only; its metadata page is "under development".
