# Bathymetry of Asia: Japan, Korea, China (with Hong Kong, Macau), Taiwan, Mongolia, South-East Asia, South Asia, Central Asia; seas, coasts, lakes, reservoirs and rivers
Run date 2026-11-13 (board item i1321, task #1321), two passes. Research only. Method: four area sub-agents per pass (Sonnet, medium effort: Japan and Korea; China, Taiwan, Mongolia and Central Asia; South-East Asia; South Asia), each given the exact table columns of the sibling reports and a row-number block; I merged their rows by script into one table per region plus one inland table (2.5). The second pass (the PM's review gaps) replaced rows in place (2, 5, 7, 8, 11, 20, 40, 42 to 47, 49, 60 to 62, 76, 77, 81, 82, 90, 110, 140, 141, 160, 165, 186) and added rows 21 to 24, 50 to 53, 78, 79, 92, 93, 142 to 147, 166, 187 and 188; I then read every row of tables 2.2 to 2.5 and the changed rows of 2.1 in full before writing the headline. The row numbers skip on purpose: blocks per area (coast 1-99, inland 100-199) keep numbers stable across passes, so a gap is unused, not lost (row 36 was deleted as unused; 2.3 rows 63 to 65 are the same products as 31 to 33 and say so). Evidence: pages or API answers fetched with a 2xx answer in the run; User-Agent `scenic-roads/0.1 (personal offline map)`; data files that decided a fact were downloaded and read whole (the Tokyo islands index ZIP, the Thai DMR Shapefile, the SCSBathy10 products ZIP, a Shizuoka LAS sheet, the NARA booklet, the Malaysian annual reports, the SO190 grid) and deleted afterwards; the 68.8 MB data.gov.tw dataset CSV was fetched once; no login, bot check or CAPTCHA bypassed. Figures that appeared only in search summaries were fetched or dropped: NAMRIA's "58 % of the EEZ" is on no NAMRIA page (only a third-party page), and Malaysia's Hydrographic Act is "in development" per one fetched Malay news report (row notes, section 3). Global products (GEBCO, GMRT, ETOPO, Allen Coral Atlas, Navionics, GLOBathy) are in bathy-global.md and only referred to; the Middle East is bathy-africa-me.md, the Caspian and Russia's Pacific coast bathy-europe-e.md (pointers only). Legal limits on geodata in general: terrain-asia.md sections 3.1 to 3.3; only what bears on depth is added here. "Not stated" means the source does not say. Row URLs are inline in the cells (section 8 lists them).
