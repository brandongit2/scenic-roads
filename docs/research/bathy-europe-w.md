Method: four area sub-agents (EMODnet/Mediterranean compilations plus UK and Ireland; France, Benelux and Alpine lakes; Iberia and the Atlantic islands; Italy, the Adriatic states, Greece, Cyprus, Turkey), each told to cite only pages or API answers fetched OK in the run; User-Agent `scenic-roads/0.1 (personal offline map)`; files opened whole where a fact needed them (see section 6). ## 1. Headline

- Pan-European base: EMODnet Bathymetry DTM 2024 (1/16 arc-minute ~115 m, LAT, 59 tiles plus a Caribbean tile; Sextant record: 'Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International', not for navigation; six sampled HR records say the same, row 1). It is the only seamless open grid for the whole region and the answer to "measured or GEBCO-filled": the open WFS layers `source_references` (197,828 features for release 2024, counted; type CDI = survey, DTM = composite, unmatched = GEBCO/IBCAO gap) and `quality_index` at `ows.emodnet-bathymetry.eu`, which also serves WCS and the `download_tiles` layer. Its `hr_bathymetry_area` layer lists 1,617 high-resolution DTM polygons (counted; resolution field 32-1024, 2018-2024), each with a download URL, and is the best open finer data for many coasts (1,561 of the 1,617 lie in this region's box (counted; no country field, only provider ids: Geological Survey Ireland 427, ICM-CSIC Spain 392, Deltares 345, BSH 85, IHPT Portugal 66, BGS 63, Ifremer 44); Iberia alone ~60 named DTMs, Portugal's Azores and Madeira well covered, none found for the Canaries) (rows 2-3, 49-50). Datum LAT: land heights (ODN, NGF, NAP, Alicante MSL) do not match without conversion.
- IBCM never became a grid (contours only); MediMap 500/1000 m (2008) is marked obsolete; the CNR-ISMAR Adriatic 200 m kriged grid is access-restricted; no Copernicus Marine bathymetry product was found (rows 5-8).
- Best open national sets: Ireland INFOMAR (10 m for 0-200 m, 25 m whole waters, 100 m offshore, LAT ImageServers, CC BY 4.0, plus a 373-polygon survey-leg coverage layer with per-leg resolution); France Shom (Licence Ouverte 2.0: 111 m facade MNT Atlantic/Gulf of Lion/Corsica, ~20 m coastal and ~5 m harbour MNTs, Litto3D 1 m/5 m as `.7z` tiles, LAT or MSL variants, the JSON listings are the tile index); Netherlands Rijkswaterstaat (CC0, NAP: 20 m coast and sea to end of 2024 with a survey-date WFS layer, 1 m multibeam of inland waters 14.5 GB, IJsselmeer); Portugal Instituto Hidrográfico (CC BY 4.0, WCS: 42 coverages at 25 m); Spain IHM MBAR24 (16/32/100 m, Nov 2025, free registration, CC BY-NC 4.0 per the licence PDF, datum unstated); Catalonia ICGC Batimetria v2.1 (1 m bathymetric-lidar topobathy to -50 m, CC BY 4.0, 2.79 GB COG).
- UK: no open UK-wide grid at survey resolution. UKHO's Seabed Mapping Service is a sign-in app (Azure B2C, key-gated WMTS) with no public survey index; UKHO's catalogue says detailed bathymetry is outside OGL where non-releasable; the open footprint for UK multibeam is BGS's Offshore GeoIndex (965 survey polygons, 57 with multibeam, 54 of those on OGL terms; row 75). Open pieces: Defra Marine DEM (6 arc-second open; 1 arc-second only by SFTP request; chart datum), Environment Agency SurfZone DEM 2 m and multibeam 50 cm with an open survey-index WMS, coastalmonitoring.org (OGL, form downloads), DAERA satellite-derived bathymetry and a lidar pilot. Crown Dependencies, Gibraltar, Lough Neagh/Erne, Loch Ness and national UK lake bathymetry share rows 77-78 (routes named); JIBS nothing. Added: AFBI lidar test sites (row 76).
- Italy: ISPRA publishes the IIM 1:250,000 isobath layer (5-200 m, whole Italy, vector, CC BY 4.0; row 90), which softens the earlier 'no public IIM download'. MaGIC (Civil Protection, 50-1000 m, 50/100/200 m grids, CC BY 4.0 on GitHub, 73-sheet footprint shapefile) is the one open multibeam product; the 0-50 m strip is only in MASE "AMP" 4 m terra-mare DTMs for four regions (CC BY 4.0, WMS confirmed, download unconfirmed), Emilia-Romagna 5 m (CC BY 4.0 on the dati.gov.it record, row 67), Liguria PTAMC isobaths (1 m model not confirmed downloadable, row 88), Toscana CAMP-Italy beach rasters (row 89) and the Venice lagoon 0.5 m (CC BY 4.0). IIM, Croatia (HHI), Slovenia, Malta, Cyprus, Albania, Montenegro, Bosnia: nothing open; Greece HNHS has a free 463 m DTM by request form; Turkey SHOD data by request form (rows 63-74).
- Lakes: swissBATHY3D (22 lakes counted, 1 m; Constance 3 m; Geneva and Maggiore 2 m; elevations in LN02, one zip per lake in swisstopo's STAC; opendata.swiss `terms_by`); Austria open under CC BY (Wörthersee 1 m, Achensee 0.5 m, Danube bed 0.25/0.5 m for the whole Austrian river, Vorarlberg Bodensee 1 m contours, Salzburg contours); Windermere 1/5 m (BGS), Welsh lakes (NRW, OGL), two Irish lakes, Lago Trasimeno. - Rivers: Danube (Austria, viadonau) and the Dutch inland waters are the open measured sets; Rhône (CNR), VNF and French lakes: nothing open found.
- Measured-coverage indexes that can feed coverage layers: EMODnet `source_references`/`quality_index`/`hr_bathymetry_area`; INFOMAR coverage service; EA survey-index WMS; Shom sounding layer `LEVES_BATHY` and the `.7z` JSON listings; Rijkswaterstaat survey-date polygons; Flanders survey grids; MaGIC `quadro-unione.shp`; Venice, Emilia-Romagna and AMP footprints from WMS.

## 3. Notes per jurisdiction

### Pan-European and Mediterranean compilations; UK, Ireland, Crown Dependencies, Gibraltar

**Europe-wide (EMODnet).** Use DTM 2024 as the sea-floor base (115 m). It is LAT, so land elevation (ODN, NGF, etc.) and sea depth do not match without a conversion; EMODnet itself only offers an MSL variant for the ESRI ASCII tiles [1][5]. Gaps are filled by GEBCO 2024 and IBCAO V4, so the `source_references` layer is what tells surveyed from filled: a polygon of type CDI or DTM is a measured source, anything unmatched is grey/GEBCO [1][6]. The WCS answers were 2.3 KB (service capabilities), `emodnet__mean` is the current release and `emodnet__mean_2022` etc. are older ones [2]. The HR-DTM layer is the best open finer data for many coasts (BGS Scottish sea lochs are in it, 63 polygons under EDMO 42, 128 = 1/128 arc-minute [8]) but is mixed in resolution and datum per record. EMODnet distributes DTM products, not the surveys: surveys are via CDI requests [1].

**Mediterranean.** The only open Mediterranean-wide grid with a fetched licence is EMODnet. IBCM never produced a grid (NCEI text says the gridded CD "should" be the next achievement; contours only on the GEBCO Digital Atlas and EPIC) [35]. MediMap (500/1000 m, 2008) is marked obsolete and points to EMODnet [36]. Adriatic: the CNR-ISMAR 200 m kriged grid is access-restricted; the Venice lagoon multibeam is open (CC BY 4.0, 1 arc-second) through EMODnet [37]. Coastal Spain/Italy/Greece/Croatia/Cyprus satellite-derived bathymetry is folded into the DTM [9]. Black Sea: covered by the same EMODnet DTM [1]; national data belong to #1319/#1320.

**Satellite-derived and Copernicus.** EMODnet's own satellite product is a coastline, not depth [1][3]. No Copernicus Marine bathymetry product was found (D).

**UK.** Best open options by purpose: (1) shelf base: EMODnet 115 m or the Defra 6 arc-second DEM (about 185 m north-south, chart datum, open) [18]; (2) coast and intertidal: EA SurfZone DEM 2 m with an open survey index, plus EA multibeam 50 cm [19][20][17]; (3) per-region monitoring: NNRCMP multibeam and bathy-topo data under OGL but form-gated downloads [23]. UKHO's holdings are the largest but the access path (a beta app) and a machine-readable survey index were not verified. Datum traps: Defra DEM Chart Datum [18]; EA products Newlyn [17][19]; EMODnet LAT [1]; NNRCMP grids move between OSGM02 and OSGM15 [23]. The UKHO is a hydrographic office and its charts' datum is Chart Datum; no site-specific value was read.
- Northern Ireland: DAERA 2021 SDB (whole NI marine area, 8-11 m deep max) and bathymetric lidar pilot (Dundrum Bay, Carlingford Lough), both OGL MapServers with footprint layers [30][31].
- Scotland: BGS sea-loch multibeam surveys exist as catalogue records (Loch Alsh/Duich, Eriboll, Lomond, Broom, Long) with viewer links only [16]. Marine Scotland's NMPi serves Rockall multibeam via token-bearing URLs [38].
- Wales: Welsh Lakes (OGL) [32]; NNRCMP hosts Welsh Coastal Monitoring Centre data [24].
- Crown Dependencies (Jersey, Guernsey, Isle of Man) and Gibraltar: nothing found in the UK catalogue (D). The UKHO S-102 trial cell for the Channel Islands exists (portal item list) [14]; Gibraltar and Channel Islands maritime-limits PDFs are on the UKHO portal [14].

**Ireland.** INFOMAR is the best source: three nested ImageServers, a queryable coverage layer carrying per-leg resolution and year, CC BY 4.0. Traps: the 10 m service covers only 0-200 m, the 100 m only 200-5000 m [26]; not every survey is downloadable [28]; the earlier survey's bbox (-20.1..-5.1, 51.5..57.1) is smaller than the three services' extents (see F). Vertical: LAT by service name, with the vertical CRS recorded as 5861 [26]. JIBS: no record found (D).

**Lakes.** Windermere (BGS/Southampton) is the only large English lake with fetched multibeam data. No UKCEH or UK Lakes Portal bathymetry set was found through the data.gov.uk catalogue (D). Loch Ness: no dataset found. Welsh lakes (NRW) and two Irish lakes are open [32][34].

**Second pass (UK, Ireland, EMODnet).** Licence: the 2024 DTM's Sextant record, field 'Access constraints': 'Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International' with 'DO NOT USE FOR NAVIGATION' [169]; EMODnet's terms page is 403. The six HR records sampled (BGS, Geological Survey Ireland, ICM-CSIC, Deltares, BSH, IHPT) all carry the same wording [172]-[177]; restrictions come from composite metadata, e.g. CNR-ISMAR-22 'Restricted' [178]. WFS axis-order trap: with WFS 2.0.0 `cql_filter=BBOX(geom,28,-32,62,37)` means latitude 28..62, longitude -32..37 (1,561 hits), while `BBOX(geom,-32,28,37,62)` gives 0; whole-world check returned 1,617 [171]. UKHO: the Seabed Mapping Service is a sign-in app, not an open tile service; the survey index and back-end are behind the login (the one permission check refused my single attempt to call the back-end, so it was not called); UKHO surveys are not a cache source, EMODnet (carries UK composites) and the Defra DEM are the open routes [179]-[184]. BGS GeoIndex Offshore is the best open footprint for UK multibeam, OGL terms per survey [185][186]. coastalmonitoring.org (the former Channel Coastal Observatory) hosts six English regional programmes plus Wales and lists single-beam, multibeam, lidar and bathy/topo airborne grids, but no index-layer endpoint could be found [195]. Northern Ireland: AFBI lidar test sites (row 76); no open Lough Neagh or Erne bathymetry [196].

### France, Monaco, Benelux, Alpine lakes and rivers

**France (incl. Corsica, Monaco).** Best open sources by zoom: facade MNT 111 m for the open sea, 20 m coastal MNT where it exists (estuaries, Channel, Morbihan, Arcachon), 5 m for harbours, Litto3D 1/5 m for the shoreline strip and shallow waters. A seamless French coverage is not offered; gaps between the 20 m boxes fall back to the 111 m facade. The facade and 20 m products are made for hydrodynamic models (tsunami TANDEM, flood HOMONIM) and compile soundings plus other data; they give no per-cell measured/interpolated flag, so the only measured-coverage index I found is the sounding layer (row 31: survey ids, WMS vector layer `LEVES_BATHY_*_WMSV`) and the Litto3D tile lists. Datum trap: PBMA = lowest astronomical tide at each point; NM = mean sea level; the two files are separate downloads, and BathyElli (row 32) is for ellipsoid conversion. Litto3D datum was not found in the records read: confirm per tile before merging with the facade. Corsica: facade MNT row 27 and Litto3D Corse 2017-2018 (5 m, 227 tiles, doi in record) [54]. Monaco has no own record found; it lies within the Gulf of Lion-Cote d'Azur facade box (to 7.9 E) and the Litto3D PACA bbox (to 7.6 E), my inference from the bboxes. The Shom pages list 122 datasets in the data.gouv organisation [39]; the third page of my listing was cut off, so a few Shom bathymetry sets may be missing from the product list. Regional observatories: Litto3D NHDF cites "Shom - ROLNHDF, 2022" (Observatoire du Littoral Normandie-Hauts-de-France), Corsica cites Collectivité de Corse and DREAL [54]; I found no separate ROL/OCA/GeoBretagne topobathy outside the Shom Litto3D sets. Rivers and lakes: no VNF, CNR, Léman-France, Annecy or Bourget bathymetry found on data.gouv.fr. DDT51 (Marne) publishes 3D bathymetric profile points of small rivers (Vesle, Ardre, Prosne ...) and DREAL Occitanie "Levés bathymétriques" on a flood-forecast service, both low value for a 3D map (profile lines, not surfaces) [74].

**Netherlands.** Everything is CC0. Datum NAP (heights, negative below), not LAT, except the separate LAT-referenced index set; the 1 m composites exclude LAT-referenced data [61]. Use row 33 for the coast and sea, row 34 for rivers and canals, row 35 for IJsselmeer. The 1 m set is 14.5 GB as one file, so use the WCS or the LOL tiles. Check the polygons layer for survey dates: the 20 m grid mixes old and new soundings [61]. JARKUS (beach profiles along raai lines) records exist (Jarkus serie, Jarkusraaien) but are profile lines, not a surface [61].

**Belgium.** Flanders' Hydrography team keeps the TRITON database since 1970; the shelf DTM v9 is 20 m, LAT, with a WCS that I could not reach [65]. Westerschelde/Zeeschelde are in the same database; the Dutch side of the estuary is in the Rijkswaterstaat sets (Westerschelde ecotope bed-height maps 1996, 2004, 2015 exist as Dutch records) [61]. Wallonia: no bathymetry sought beyond terrain lidar (see terrain report).

**Luxembourg.** data.public.lu has no bathymetry dataset for Haute-Sûre; searches for "bathymétrie", "lac haute-sûre", "profondeur lac" and "bathymetry" returned 0 or only zoning records [72].

**Switzerland and Liechtenstein.** swissBATHY3D is the best source for 22 lakes: whole Lake Constance (all countries) at 3 m, and Lake Geneva and Maggiore at 2 m with partial coverage in French and Italian waters [67]. Datum is elevation in LN02, so depth = lake level minus elevation; lake levels differ and change [67]. Liechtenstein has no lake. Cantonal/Eawag sets: not found separately (see D).

**Austria.** Open measured sets: Wörthersee, Achensee, Danube, Vorarlberg Bodensee, Salzburg contours (rows 39-43). Not found: Lake Neusiedl, Attersee, Traunsee, Mondsee, Millstätter See as DTMs in the portal search [69]. The German side of Lake Constance: pointer only (IGKB, see C and D).

**Italy's great lakes.** Nothing open found for Garda, Como, Maggiore (Italian part) in dati.gov.it (searches "batimetria", "batimetrico", "lago di Garda/Como/Maggiore"). Only Trasimeno [71] and Adriatic/Tuscan marine sets (outside this area).

**Second pass (France, Benelux, Alpine).** Litto3D: heights are IGN69 altitudes (IGN78 for Corsica), positive up, not depths, so merging with PBMA-referenced Shom MNT needs a location-dependent offset that was not sourced; tiles are 1 km named by the lower-left km corner, the per-group JSON listing (with size and MD5) is the tile index [198][199][201]. The Shom coastal MNT is interpolated (row 28), so the only measured index is the sounding layer (row 31). Léman: not wholly covered by swissBATHY3D (row 38). Belgian shelf DTM v9: metadata record read, download host unreachable (row 36); Port of Antwerp-Bruges 1 m ImageServer added (row 79). Annecy, Bourget, Rhône, VNF, estuaries (Seine, Loire) and Luxembourg: shared row 80 names the routes. The Gironde is covered by the Shom 20 m MNT.

### Spain, Portugal, Atlantic islands, Andorra

**Spain, national.** Two open-ish routes exist and the earlier survey's "nothing found" no longer holds. (1) IHM MBAR24 at cdihm.cnig.es [92][93][94][95]: 16-100 m, newest and the finest national seabed grid, but gated: login, declared purpose, and CC BY-NC 4.0 text. The platform's FAQ says commercial use is allowed with credit [94] while the licence PDF forbids it [93]; the licence PDF (dated 7 Apr 2025 on the policy page [92]) governs. Datum is not stated anywhere I could read, so a LAT/MSL/hydrographic-zero trap is open; the IHM national report says the IHM is designing a Hydrographic Reference Surface to tie bathymetry to a hydrographic zero [96]. The IHM was part of EMODnet only from 2013 to June 2016 [96], so EMODnet Spain is older IHM data. (2) MAPA's ESPACE shelf contours (CC BY 4.0, vectors, 2001-04) [114] and the IEO isobaths record (IDEE, "any use with IEO credit" per search, not fetched). MAPA also publishes fishing-area isobath files built on EMODnet-2022 data [120]; not useful as bathymetry. IEO multibeam ZEE grids: no downloadable grid found; the only evidence is search snippets (ZEE 2025 campaign, EM124), not usable (D).

**Spain, coastal strip.** MITECO ecocartografias (multibeam, 2008-2012, province pages with KMZ zips) [109][110][111], no licence stated: usable as a lead and for footprints, caching status unknown. Catalonia is the standout: ICGC bathymetric lidar 1 m, CC BY 4.0, MSL Alicante datum [100][101]. Andalucia: 20 m land-sea DEM referenced to the Alicante zero [115]; Cadiz 1:1,000/1:5,000 topobathy [117]. Basque Country: only 1/5 m isobaths from 2009 verified [98]; the terrain report lists the Basque 1 m land MDT (WCS) [121] but no topobathy grid was verified. Balearics: IDW 10 m CSIC grid to 40 m, CC BY [99][103][104]; the IDEIB catalogue gave no bathymetry layer in a search that returned nothing (D). Canaries: GRAFCAN 2.5 m topobathy as WMS only [119]; MITECO ecocartografias exist for Las Palmas and Tenerife [111]. Gulf of Cadiz and Alboran: EMODnet HR-DTMs 1/32'-1/64' [106].

**Datum traps (Spain).** Land datum is mean sea level at Alicante (Catalonia states it with EGM08D595 [100], Andalucia states the Alicante zero [115]); charts and the EMODnet DTM use LAT [107]; Canaries' own record says land and sea sit on different zeros [119]. Merging ICGC/Andalucia heights with EMODnet depths without a correction will step at the shoreline.

**Portugal.** The IH is the open one: dados.gov.pt lists about 38 "Modelo Batimetrico" sets plus the ZEE model, all CC BY, served by WCS from webgeo1.hidrografico.pt [75][76][78][80]. The ZEE model is only the EMODnet 1/16' resample [75], while the 25 m sets are the real IH detail [78][85]. 2011 coastal lidar (400 m land, 600 m sea, 2 m) is real but by-request [83][84]. Azores: regional vector bathymetry CC BY [81][82][86], EMODnet HR-DTMs for every island except Flores/Corvo (not in the filter, counted) [106], and the 2024 lidar from the terrain report (land). Madeira: EMODnet HR_Madeira_IlhaMadeira and PortoSanto [106] plus IH 25 m models (Canical, Garajau, Porto Moniz, Porto Santo) [78]. Datum for all IH models not stated in the records [87][88].

**Reservoirs and lakes.** Nothing open at national scale in either country (search of dados.gov.pt for albufeira/barragem bathymetry returned only the Lagoa de Albufeira study (a coastal lagoon, PDF/CSW) [80], and datos.gob.es "embalse" titles are state/inventory only [97]). Open exceptions: Rules reservoir (Granada), lidar DEM 2008 with CC BY 4.0, bed coverage unclear [116]; Azores lagoons (11 named) as vector contours [82]; Alqueva: EDIA offers free ortho and altimetry data but bathymetry is not confirmed [122].

**Andorra.** No sea or large lake; the national 15 m MDT is rights-reserved (terrain report). No lake bathymetry found (D).

**Second pass (Iberia).** IEO is not 'nothing found': the national INSPIRE CSW at idee.es (360 'batimetr' hits, counted) lists the 100 m national depth model, 22 INTEMARES multibeam grids and 26 multibeam coverage polygons, all CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, accessed only through the IEO viewer; the polygons are the best 'measured multibeam' footprint for Spanish waters (rows 81-83) [220]-[224]. MBAR24 licence conflict: both texts quoted in row 46; datum unstated in every source. Basque Country, Galicia, Asturias, Cantabria, Murcia, Valencia, confederations/CEDEX, Puertos del Estado, Alqueva: row 62 and row 85. EMEPC multibeam WMS exists but is restricted (row 86).
