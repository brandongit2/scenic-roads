# North American bathymetry: USA, Canada, Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean (oceans, coasts, lakes, reservoirs, rivers)
Run date 2026-11-13 (board item i1316, task #1316). Research only. Method: four area sub-agents (US federal and Atlantic/Gulf states; US Pacific, Alaska, Hawaii, territories and inland waters; Canada; Mexico, Central America, Caribbean), each told to cite only pages or API answers fetched in the run; User-Agent `scenic-roads/0.1 (personal offline map)`; files were fetched whole only where a row names what was read from them. Figures are the publishers' own; "(counted)" or "(mine)" marks a tally from a fetched index or API answer. Global products (GEBCO, GMRT, ETOPO, DCDB, Navionics) are in bathy-global.md and only referred to here. A second pass closed gaps flagged in review (US lakes, NOAA volumes and tile coverage, Alaska, Oregon, Texas, inland states, Canada, Mexico and the Caribbean); its findings are folded into the tables, so each product has one row.

## 1. Headline

- Best US coastal source: NOAA BlueTopo (bucket `noaa-ocs-nationalbathymetry-pds`, 8,690 tiles, ~97 GB, 3-band GeoTIFF with elevation, vertical uncertainty and a contributor raster attribute table; pixel size 2/4/8/16 m by tile prefix, NAVD88, public domain). The tile scheme has 12,638 polygons, of which 8,689 are delivered (read whole). NOAA's FAQ says only the Gulf, Atlantic and Caribbean regions are complete, yet the delivered tiles already include California (about 1,341), Southeast Alaska and the Gulf of Alaska coast (about 2,209) and Puerto Rico/USVI (about 227); the scheme has none for Hawaii, Guam or the Great Lakes, and none delivered for Oregon or Washington (about 1,087 scheme tiles, undelivered). Measured vs interpolated is recoverable per cell (`bathy_coverage`). Row 1; datum caveats in 3.1. eHydro has no datum field in the layer; datums were read per survey for 22 of 37 districts.
- Seamless 1 arc-second base including deep water: NCEI Coastal Relief Model 2023 Vol 1-5 (Atlantic and Gulf, EGM2008, NetCDF 1.5-1.7 GB each). The 2023+ folder `crm/cudem/` also holds Vol 7 and 8 (Pacific coast, 2025), Vol 9 (Puerto Rico) and Vol 10 (Hawaii); there is no new Vol 6 and southern Alaska stays 24 arc-second (2009). The old 3 arc-second volumes are superseded. CUDEM 1/9 and 1/3 arc-second (NAVD88; per-cell "spatial metadata" zips of 790 MB and 273 MB) cover the Atlantic/Gulf block, and Hawaii, Puerto Rico/USVI, Guam, CNMI and American Samoa are in the same bucket with island datums (GUVD04, ASVD02). USGS CoNED 1 m models cover the Carolinas, Georgia and the northern Gulf (spatial metadata per cell).
- Measured-coverage indexes that feed coverage layers (all open ArcGIS or file indexes): NOS survey BAG footprints (2,988 surveys with BAGs), NCEI multibeam sounding-density layers, eHydro (124,182 survey jobs), UNH CCOM footprints, Florida FCMaP footprints, the PacIOOS allDatasets CSV (bbox of every Hawaii and territory grid), the NRCan/DFO west-coast DEM "Data Source Map", the CHS NONNA index shapefiles (72,305 + 1,220 cells) and the CHS Survey Index (7,078 polygons, TECSOU and CATZOC, but it ends in 2016), Ontario's 11,517-lake bathymetry index and Quebec's 3,280-lake GBLQ index.
- Best state/regional data finer than federal: California State Waters Map Series (2 m, USGS, NAVD88, public domain); San Francisco Bay 1 m MLLW DEMs (USGS); Oregon State Waters mapping is NOS surveys H12122-H12129, downloadable as variable-resolution MLLW BAGs (OSU's own host is dead); Hawaii main islands 50 m and northwest islands 60 m synthesis plus Guam, CNMI, American Samoa 1-60 m grids (UH SOEST / NOAA PIBHMC via PacIOOS, free redistribution, not for navigation); Gulf of Maine to Long Island 2-16 m multibeam synthesis (UNH CCOM, image services only); Long Island Sound 1 m (UConn/Stony Brook; MGDS archive); Alaska: AFSC Aleutians 100 m `aigrid100m.zip` (615 MB) and about 55 NCEI Alaska DEMs (MHHW/MHW datums); Texas: TNRIS StratMap bay and lidar collections (CC0 per their record) whose file host returned 403 to our User-Agent; Washington DNR 1 m MLLW copy of CoNED (a VDatum transform of the federal parent). Most other state portals only republish federal data or old contours (section 3.1).
- Great Lakes: NCEI's five lake grids are all 3 arc-second (~90 m), interpolated from hand contours, in each lake's own low-water datum (NOT IGLD85 by name); Superior is an incomplete draft. Newer measured data are patches: NOAA 2019 Finger Lakes and Lake Champlain topobathy lidar (1 m, NAVD88), USGS seamless topobathy DEMs (2025), USGS local multibeam, the Ontario shoreline topobathy DEM and CHS NONNA cells on the Canadian side (index cells: Superior ~761, Ontario ~300, Georgian Bay ~263, Erie ~179). NOAA says 85% of the US Great Lakes are not mapped to modern standards.
- Canada: CHS NONNA-10/-100 (Chart Datum, 10 m / 100 m, WGS84, updated April 2026; the WCS band is numeric depth, negative down) is the national backbone. Licence, read in full (3.5): the CKAN record is labelled OGL-Canada but itself says the CHS NONNA Licence governs the data; open.canada.ca offers no direct bathymetry download, so every data route (the guest-login portal, WMS/WCS/WMTS) falls under the NONNA Licence by its clauses 1 and 12; OGL-Canada covers the record and the small index files. Clause 2 says CHS copyright "shall not be sold, licensed, leased, assigned or given to a third party"; clause 14 requires destroying all copies within 15 days of a CHS public termination notice; clause 7 requires a CHS notice on derivatives; clause 3 bars navigation. The WMS/WCS capabilities say Fees and AccessConstraints NONE. A personal cache is plausible but carries these duties: the owner decides. No per-cell measured flag (contributor detail only inside the CSAR/ASCII downloads). Other open grids: NRCan/DFO west-coast 10 m topobathy DEM (datum not stated), GSC Atlantic 100 m compilation (2023), CHS 500 m grids, Magdalen lagoons 5 m, Salish Sea 80 m DEM (OGL, WMS open); NCEI hosts 10 raw CCGS Amundsen multibeam surveys; Lake Winnipeg 860.6 m grid via CanWIN WCS (licence conflict CC BY-SA vs OGL-Canada).
- Lakes: Quebec GBLQ (CC BY 4.0, 3,280 lakes, depth below water surface), Ontario (points, contours, index; OGL), Alberta (~250 lakes, 50 m DEMs), BC (2,741 scanned maps; 26 lakes as digitised contours in the public WFS), Indiana (145 lakes, ArcGIS REST), New Hampshire (9,285 contour lines), Minnesota (DNR lakes now served as ArcGIS Hub layers), Michigan (inland contour layers, 25,492 features, open ArcGIS services), Ohio (134 inland lakes plus the Erie service), Texas TWDB (~123 reservoirs, shapefiles, NAVD88 tables). USGS 0.5-1 m topobathy DEMs: Great Salt Lake (NAVD88, 34 GB GeoTIFF, no rights statement), Bear Lake (NAVD88, CC0), Lake Powell 1 m (NAVD88) and Powell 2017 2 m multibeam (NAVD88, CC0, 414 MB); Lake Mead 10 m (OFR 03-320, datum not stated); Lake Tahoe 1998 USGS multibeam 10 m, reachable only through Wayback; Crater Lake 2 m multibeam (public domain, no datum named); Yellowstone Lake release holds no bathymetry grid. Nothing public found for Shasta, Oroville (the 2022 multibeam grid is not public), Pend Oreille (map only), Flathead, a TERC Tahoe product, Kootenay Lake, Chapala, Nicaragua, Gatun or Izabal grids. Great Bear / Great Slave are only 1 km depth classes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Missouri has a USGS multibeam series, Colorado USAFA DEMs, Walker Lake NV 5 m (table 2.7).
- Mexico and Central America: no national bathymetric grid found. INEGI's CEM 3.0 is land only (15 m). INEGI's sea-floor product is a public WFS/WMS of 1:1,000,000 bathymetric contours (6,671 features, lon -101 to -84.8, lat 12.1 to 26.0; the Mexican Pacific coast west of 101W is not in it; the page's own download is CAPTCHA-gated and was not used). SEMAR sells charts only (printed or raster BSB). CONABIO has only a Puerto Morelos reef depth layer. Lake Atitlan 2014 survey: paper only, no grid. Usable open sets: GEBCO/GMRT (global report), EMODnet DTM 2024 Caribbean tile (70W-60W, 11N-19N, ~115 m, CC BY 4.0), Allen Coral Atlas 10 m satellite-derived depth, research grids (Gulf of California, CC BY-NC-SA).
- Caribbean: French Antilles are best served (Shom 100 m MNTs and Litto3D 1 m bathymetric lidar for Guadeloupe and Martinique, Licence Ouverte; the Shom catalogue JSON API needs no login and can build a tile index); Dutch Caribbean WCS (Public Domain Mark); Turks and Caicos government portal (CC BY SDB and benthic maps); Bermuda NOAA NCEI DEM; Grenada NCEI 1 arc-second DEM (2017, 121 MB), the only non-US-territory island state in NOAA's footprint layer besides Bermuda; Puerto Rico and USVI have CRM Vol 9 and CUDEM tiles. Cuba, Jamaica, Haiti, Dominican Republic, Bahamas, Cayman, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados and the OECS states: one more route each (UKHO Seabed Mapping is login-gated and "around the UK"; Commonwealth Marine Economies surveyed Grenada and St Vincent and the Grenadines without a public download; TNC CaribNode did not connect) found nothing; they share one row in 2.9.
- Open for a personal offline cache (licence read): everything NOAA/NCEI/USGS/BOEM (public domain; "not for navigation"; Powell 2017 and Bear Lake explicitly CC0), PacIOOS (free redistribution), OGL-Canada records and index files, Quebec GBLQ (CC BY), EMODnet (CC BY 4.0), Shom (Licence Ouverte), Dutch WCS (PDM), Turks and Caicos copy (CC BY), TNRIS bay collections (CC0, host blocked), CSUMB SFML library (non-commercial only), research grids CC BY-NC-SA (non-commercial, share-alike). Conditional: CHS NONNA data (NONNA Licence duties in 3.5). Not open or unclear: Middlebury Lake Champlain multibeam, CRI Mactaquac composite (permission needed), OSU Oregon web map (restrictive licence), UKHO seabed service (login), OSM/OpenSeaMap depth, Allen Coral Atlas terms (pages 404), Polar Data Catalogue terms (v2.1: personal use only, no downloading or mirroring unless a CC licence is attached), Great Salt Lake (no rights statement).
- Earlier survey (RESEARCH-BATHYMETRY.md): its North American claims were mostly confirmed; two were contradicted or corrected (Great Lakes spacing, NONNA CKAN date); the former "not confirmed" items are now mostly resolved (section 7). Still open: section 5 (blocked or absent portals: Saskatchewan token wall, PEI Radware wall, NWT 504, Nunavut 401, gis-hub.ca login, TNRIS 403, OSU bot challenge).

## 3. Notes per jurisdiction

### 3.1 US federal and Atlantic/Gulf

- **Federal first choice**: BlueTopo tiles for everything shallow and navigable (NOAA's FAQ lists only the Gulf, Atlantic and Caribbean regions as complete, but the bucket already holds California, Southeast Alaska and Puerto Rico/USVI tiles; see the BlueTopo row), CRM 2023 for a seamless 30 m base including deep water (Vol 1-5 Atlantic/Gulf; Vol 7 and 8 Pacific coast, 2025; Vol 9 Puerto Rico and USVI; Vol 10 Hawaii; no 2023+ Vol 6, so Southern California stays 2003 at 3 arc-second, and Alaska has only the 2009 24 arc-second Southern Alaska grid), BOEM only for the north-Gulf seismic-derived deeper sea floor, CUDEM 1/9 and 1/3 and CoNED for the land-water margin. NOS BAGs and Digital Coast lidar are source layers for BlueTopo; fetch them only if finer than 4 m is wanted.
- **CRM and CUDEM detail**: the NCEI CRM product page lists ten volumes plus three older special grids and says the models cover "the continental U.S., Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico" [13]; the 2023+ files sit in the THREDDS folder `crm/cudem/` (crm_vol1..5_2023, crm_vol9_2023, crm_vol10_2023, crm_vol7_2025, crm_vol8_2025) [18][14]. The 2025 guide names NCEI CUDEM 1/9 arc-second (NAVD88, 2012-2025) and BlueTopo (NAVD88) among the Pacific volumes' sources [12]. CUDEM tiles are 0.25-degree, 8112 px per side at 1/9 arc-second; every ISO record says "Not to be used for navigation" [27]. Other measured Digital Coast DEM project folders sit in the same bucket (525 in all, e.g. `CA_Central_CoNED_DEM_2017_8657`, `Puget_Sound_CoNED_DEM_2020_9112`, `HI_BigIsland_DEM_2023_10336`, `NGS_Guam_topobathy_DEM_2020_9323`, `NGS_PR_Topobathy_DEM_2019_9392`); their datums and spacing were not read except the Puget Sound CoNED one [25].
- **eHydro**: points only; the datum is per survey and varies inside a district (Great Lakes IGLD85, Mississippi and Missouri river-specific planes or NAVD88, coastal MLLW); see the eHydro row.
- **Datum mismatch warning**: BlueTopo = NAVD88 [3]; CRM 2023 = EGM2008 [12]; NOS BAGs, ENC and the old Chesapeake DEM = MLLW; CUDEM, CoNED, USGS 3DEP = NAVD88. A seamless map must pick one reference or accept offsets of 0.5-1 m in tidal estuaries.
- **ME / NH / MA / RI / CT / NY**: the UNH CCOM synthesis (2 m to 16 m) is the best single Northeast multibeam source and has a footprint layer; MCMI covers Kennebunk-Matinicus in state waters; LISMaRC gives 1 m in Long Island Sound; USGS 10 m MassBay_10m and 13 m Stellwagen grids are open. State portals otherwise republish federal data or old contours (CT 1996 contours, NJ contours from CRM, Alabama contours from 2010 DEM).
- **MD / VA**: nothing better than BlueTopo + CoNED; the older bay-wide 30 m DEM (1859-1993 surveys) is worse. VIMS SHOAL crowdsourced trackline data is the only new state-run source; no download seen.
- **NC / SC / GA**: CoNED 1 m (2022-2023) is the finest seamless, with a source-per-cell metadata gpkg; Digital Coast has post-Helene 2025 lidar for NC; no state multibeam portal found.
- **FL**: FCMaP is a coordination hub; usable output is footprints and gap layers (feeds the map's coverage layer), not rasters.
- **AL / MS**: no state grid beyond contours; USGS Grand Bay and Digital Coast post-Michael DEMs.
- **LA**: CPRA CIMS has per-project survey zips; the finest integrated product is USGS NGOM-2 1 m (491 GB COG).
- **TX**: TWDB data are reservoir surveys, not bays (its bay survey pages hold only site navigation). The state's coastal bathymetry is at TNRIS (StratMap): its collection API lists nine bathymetry collections, licence field Creative Commons Zero v1.0, partly named as TWDB partner/user data [62]; the download host `data.geographic.texas.gov` answered HTTP 403 "Request blocked" to the project User-Agent for every file, so sizes come from the API and the block was not worked round [340]. TGLO and the Harte Research Institute: hosts do not resolve or the product page is 404. Otherwise bays depend on NOAA.


### 3.2 US Pacific, Alaska, Hawaii, Great Lakes, inland

- Great Lakes: all five NCEI grids are on one 3 arc-sec lattice, contour-derived, so 90 m is the effective ceiling and modern multibeam is not in them (Ontario excepted). The vertical reference is each lake's low water datum, not IGLD85 as such; Grid Extract output is MSL [65]. Superior is a draft and the weakest. Useful modern layers are the 2019 NOAA lidar (four lakes) and USGS seamless topobathy.
- California: the CSMP 2 m grids are the high-value set; USGS hosts them (public domain), while the CSUMB original library has a "no for-profit use without permission" note [76]. SF Bay is a separate 1 m MLLW DEM series.
- Hawaii/territories: one THREDDS+ERDDAP server (PacIOOS) serves every island grid with bbox in one CSV, which can build the coverage layer directly. Most of these are 2008-2011 vintage and were repackaged in 2015. NOAA's own CRM Vol 9/10 and CUDEM tiles for Puerto Rico, USVI, Hawaii, Guam, CNMI and American Samoa are in table 2.1, and the NCEI coastal DEMs for Puerto Rico and USVI in 2.9.
- Washington: DNR's 1 m MLLW surface is a VDatum transform of the USGS CoNED parent, not a new survey; the USGS Strait of Juan de Fuca topobathy model (1891-2019) is in the Pacific table. Washington lakes were not searched.
- Oregon: the "State Waters" mapping is NOAA NOS surveys H12122-H12129, downloadable as variable-resolution MLLW BAGs; OSU's own host is dead and its ArcGIS web map (restrictive licence) is the surviving index; OSU ScholarsArchive sits behind a bot challenge (not bypassed).
- Alaska: NOAA's 20 m REST bathymetry is documented but the service is gone. The AFSC Aleutians 100 m grid is `aigrid100m.zip` (615 MB; the old link is dead), with Zimmermann's Cook Inlet, Norton Sound and Central GOA smooth-sheet compilations as technical memoranda; about 55 NCEI regional DEMs cover Alaska (MHHW/MHW datums). DGGS holds only small single-beam village datasets; AOOS has no bathymetry grid found. The ScienceBase "Arctic Bathymetry (batharcst)" item is a 2003 GIS layer, not a grid, and is not used.
- Inland: state lake programmes deliver contours, not grids (MN 5 m DEM excepted); NH, IN, MI, OH, MN, ME and VT are ArcGIS REST/Hub layers, which is the easiest route for a pipeline. USGS "TBDEM" products give lake-wide 0.5-1 m DEMs for Great Salt Lake (no rights statement), Bear Lake (CC0) and Lake Powell, plus the Powell 2017 2 m multibeam (CC0). Missouri, Colorado (Air Force Academy lakes), Walker Lake and Pyramid Lake have USGS releases (see the inland table). The Lake Mead surface blends NED land elevation, so it includes above-water terrain.


### 3.3 Canada

**Federal / CHS.** NONNA is the one national product and is clearly the backbone: guest access, WCS/WMS open, three shapefile indexes small enough to keep. Counting its own index cells with my own parse of the NONNA10 shapefile names (mine, [193]): 17,667 of 72,305 cells lie at 68N or north, so the Arctic is well populated at index level; cells in rough boxes: Salish Sea ~280, Bay of Fundy ~247, Georgian Bay ~263, Lake Superior ~761, Lake Ontario ~300, Erie ~179, St Lawrence Montreal to Lake Ontario ~112, Beaufort ~2,732, Lake Winnipeg 3 to 11 (box-dependent), Great Slave 1, Great Bear 0, Lake of the Woods 7, Lake Nipigon 0, Okanagan 0. Note these are index cells (a cell exists), not fill. Cell presence does not say how much of the cell has data: the CHS says voids exist where data was removed because it was not on Chart Datum [189]. There is no published per-cell measured/interpolated flag; the only source evidence is CATZOC/TECSOU inside the downloaded CSAR/ASCII files [189][190]. For a pre-download coverage layer use the survey index [199] (stale, ends 2016) plus the NONNA10 cell shapefile [193].
Licence: the CKAN record is labelled OGL-Canada but itself says the CHS NONNA Licence governs the data; see note 3.5 for the quoted clauses and which text applies to which route.

**Great Lakes (Canadian side).** NONNA covers the lakes (cell counts above). NOAA NCEI lake grids (joint with CHS; no copyright in US) are interpolated from contours and use each lake's LWD; Lake Superior is 3 arc-second [215]. ### 3.5 NONNA licence, read in full

**The two texts.** The CKAN record (package d3881c4c-650d-4070-bf9b-1e00aabf0a1d) has `license_id: ca-ogl-lgo`, title "Open Government Licence - Canada" [187]. Its own notes add: "The Canadian Hydrographic Service (CHS) NONNA Data products are for NON-NAVIGATIONAL USE ONLY. Please see the Open Data Licence below and the CHS NONNA LICENCE viewable on https://data.chs-shc.ca/login and downloadable with the NONNA data, for the full terms and conditions governing the use of this data." [187] So the record itself names both texts as governing.

**CHS NONNA Licence** (16 clauses, EN/FR, 4 pages, `CHS_NONNA_LICENCE-LICENCE_NONNA_DU_SHC.pdf`, URL in [197], a resource of the CKAN record):

- Clause 1: "This is a non-exclusive licence between the Parties for the access and use of the CHS Data delivered via the CHS website."
- Clause 2 (third parties): "The copyright of CHS in the Data, and any permissions granted to CHS, remain the property of CHS and shall not be sold, licensed, leased, assigned or given to a third party." The same sentence is repeated in the compulsory notice of clause 7. Literal reading: it is about the copyright and permissions staying with CHS; a stricter reading takes it as a ban on the user passing the data on. A personal cache that is never handed on is outside both readings, but nothing in the text carves out personal copies.
- Clause 3: "The Data shall not be used for navigation."
- Clause 7: derivative products must carry a notice "in a prominent location on such derivative product" ("This product was made by [User] and contains intellectual property of the Canadian Hydrographic Service ... does not meet the requirements of the Navigation Safety Regulations, 2020 ...").
- Clause 12 (when it binds): "This Licence comes into effect on the date the User confirms acceptance by clicking 'I Agree' or otherwise accesses or uses the Data and remains in effect until the date it is terminated unilaterally by CHS through a public notice posted on the CHS website."
- Clause 6: "The User shall monitor the CHS website for important information and notices relevant to this Licence including its termination."
- Clause 13: "All restrictions on the use of the Data set out in this Licence survive termination."
- Clause 14 (destroying copies): "The User shall destroy all copies of the Data in their possession within 15 days of termination of this Licence."
- Clause 16: law of Ontario. Contact on every page: chsinfo@dfo-mpo.gc.ca [197].

**Which governs which route** (what the fetched texts show, not legal advice):

| Route | What the record or service says | Which text applies |
|---|---|---|
| Portal `https://data.chs-shc.ca/` (CKAN resource 1; accept terms, "Log in as guest", cart downloads of GeoTIFF/XYZ/CSAR/BAG) | The Guidance Document: read the Terms of use, check "I understand and accept the terms of use", press "Log in as guest" [190]. The portal page is an Angular app, so the on-screen Terms text is not in the static HTML and I cannot confirm it is word-for-word the PDF [345] | The NONNA Licence (explicit acceptance, clause 12). OGL-Canada is also the record's label |

There is **no direct bathymetry file download on open.canada.ca**: every data resource is the portal or an OGC service, so any NONNA depth data, from any listed route, falls under the NONNA Licence by its own clauses 1 and 12 as well as under the OGL label. For a personal offline cache the practical consequences are: (1) keep it personal (clause 2); (2) put the clause 7 notice on anything derived (a rendered map layer counts as a derivative product on a plain reading); (3) be able to delete every copy within 15 days of a CHS public termination notice, which needs a standing watch on the CHS site (clauses 6 and 14); (4) navigation use stays forbidden (clause 3). To remove the doubt, the only place that can answer which text prevails is CHS: chsinfo@dfo-mpo.gc.ca.
